PREBUILT IEC LESSON PLAN

Big Team 3 — Unit 1: Jobs

Focus: Jobs vocabulary and simple spoken introductions.

Duration 90 minutes
Periods 2 × 45 minutes
Level Beginner ELL
Skills Listening / Speaking
LESSON OBJECTIVES

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Recognize at least five of six target job words when shown pictures.
  2. Repeat at least four job words intelligibly with visual or verbal support.
  3. Use the sentence pattern "I'm a ..." to introduce a job.
  4. Produce two simple job sentences with appropriate scaffolding.
TARGET LANGUAGE

Vocabulary:

Chef · Dancer · Doctor · Firefighter · Pilot · Teacher

Sentence structure:

Teacher: "I'm a doctor."

Students: "I'm a doctor."

Teacher: "I'm a pilot."

Students: "I'm a pilot."

MATERIALS REQUIRED

Big Team 3 Student Book, Activity Book, corresponding publisher audio, six job flashcards or pictures, board, markers, and optional presentation slides.

Teacher preparation: Open the appropriate Student Book and Activity Book pages. Confirm the publisher audio tracks using the approved teacher resources before class.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

Engage · Model · Guided Practice

1. Warm-up and Objective Discussion 10 minutes

Teacher procedure:

Greet students with a smile and simple familiar language.

"Hello!"

"How are you?"

"Look!"

Show pictures of a doctor, teacher, and pilot. Ask students to name any familiar jobs.

Accept pointing, gestures, or single-word responses.

Student activity:

Look at pictures, repeat known words, and respond to the teacher.

Scaffolding: Use pictures and gestures before introducing new spoken language.
2. Instruct and Model 20 minutes

Teacher procedure:

Introduce all six jobs using flashcards. Point to each picture and say the word slowly and clearly.

"Look. Doctor."

"Listen. Doctor."

"Say: Doctor."

"Good!"

Repeat for chef, dancer, firefighter, pilot, and teacher.

Play the relevant publisher audio, pausing for students to point and repeat.

Model the structure: "I'm a doctor."

Student activity:

Listen, point, repeat vocabulary, and imitate short sentences.

Simple CCQs:

Show a doctor picture: "Doctor or pilot?"

Show a chef: "Cook?" — "Yes!"

Show a pilot: "Fly?" — "Yes!"

Show a dancer: "Dance?" — "Yes!"

Scaffolding: Picture → Word → Repetition → Sentence
3. Guided Practice 15 minutes

Teacher procedure:

Place job pictures on the board. Demonstrate matching each picture with its spoken job word.

Model a short partner activity.

Teacher: "Doctor."

Students point to doctor.

Teacher: "I'm a doctor."

Students repeat.

Student activity:

Match pictures, repeat vocabulary, and practice job sentences with a partner.

Simple ICQs:

"Point?" — "Yes!"

"One or two?" — "Two!"

"Say it?" — "Yes!"

Teacher check: Ask one pair to demonstrate before the whole class begins.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

Communicate · Apply · Assess

4. Independent Practice 30 minutes

Activity A — Song and Movement (8 min)

Play the relevant Unit 1 song. Encourage students to sing or repeat job words and use gestures.

Activity B — Activity Book (10 min)

Students complete the selected matching or tracing tasks from the Activity Book.

Demonstrate the first item on the board.

Simple ICQs:

"Draw a line?" — "Yes!"

"Write?" — "Yes!" (for tracing tasks)

"Alone?" — "Yes!"

Activity C — Job Role-play (12 min)

Give students job flashcards. Students pretend to have the job shown on their card.

Student A: "I'm a chef."

Student B: "I'm a pilot."

Rotate cards and repeat.

Scaffolding:

Step 1: Show the picture.

Step 2: Say the word.

Step 3: Say "I'm a ..."

Step 4: Speak to a partner.

5. Assessment and Closure 15 minutes

Teacher procedure:

Show job flashcards one at a time. Ask students to identify the jobs.

Invite students to produce two simple sentences.

"Look. What job?"

Student: "Doctor."

"Say: I'm a doctor."

Student: "I'm a doctor."

Give supportive feedback and praise students for participation.

Record which students need further vocabulary practice.

Exit ticket: Each student points to or names one job and attempts one complete sentence.
DIFFERENTIATED INSTRUCTION

Students Needing Support

  • Use picture cards.
  • Allow pointing first.
  • Repeat vocabulary slowly.
  • Use choral repetition.
  • Provide sentence frames.
  • Pair with a supportive peer.

Students Ready for Extension

  • Name all six jobs.
  • Produce complete sentences.
  • Practice without picture prompts.
  • Ask and answer simple job questions.
  • Help model pronunciation.
IEC FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT
Assessment Area Success Criteria Evidence
Vocabulary Recognition 5 of 6 jobs Picture identification
Pronunciation 4 of 6 job words intelligible Oral repetition
Sentence Production 2 complete sentences with support Role-play / teacher observation
Participation Participates in supported activities Teacher observation
TEACHER SUPPORT — SIMPLIFIED ICQs & CCQs
Purpose Teacher Language Expected Response
ICQ — Listening "Listen or write?" "Listen."
ICQ — Speaking "Say it?" "Yes."
ICQ — Pair Work "One or two?" "Two."
ICQ — Matching "Draw a line?" "Yes."
CCQ — Doctor "Doctor or pilot?" "Doctor."
CCQ — Chef "Cook?" "Yes."
CCQ — Pilot "Fly?" "Yes."
CCQ — Dancer "Dance?" "Yes."
Important for beginner ELL learners: Demonstrate before asking an ICQ. Use pictures and gestures for CCQs. Accept one-word answers when appropriate. Gradually build toward complete sentences.
