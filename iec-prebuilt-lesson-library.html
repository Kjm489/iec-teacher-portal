LESSON INFORMATION & OBJECTIVES
Program: Big Team 3 · English
Unit: 1 — Jobs
Lessons: 1–2 (sample pairing)
Duration: 90 minutes (2 × 45)
Skills: Listening · Speaking · Early literacy
Level: Emerging / Pre-A1-oriented (verify)

Materials & Target Language

Student Book Unit 1 job illustrations; Activity Book matching and tracing; job picture cards; relevant publisher audio.

Vocabulary: doctor, teacher, pilot, chef, dancer, firefighter.

Structure: "I'm a ..."

Measurable Learning Objectives

  • Identify at least 5 of 6 jobs from pictures.
  • Pronounce at least 4 of 6 job words intelligibly.
  • Produce two "I'm a ..." sentences with a recorded support level.

Proposed IEC lesson targets, not publisher-defined pass standards.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Objective Discussion · 10 min

Teacher: Greet students, show doctor and teacher pictures, and ask "Doctor or teacher?" Introduce today's goal: name jobs and say "I'm a ..."

Students: Point, identify familiar jobs, and repeat the target language.

Scaffold: Pictures, gestures, teacher modeling.

Optional game: Guess the Job (5 min).

2. Instruct & Model · 20 min

Teacher: Present six job pictures. Say each word, model pronunciation, play relevant listening audio, and introduce "I'm a doctor."

CCQs: Show pilot and chef pictures. "Who flies?" [Pilot]. "Who cooks?" [Chef]. Students point or say one word.

Optional game: Listen, Point, Act (5 min).

3. Guided Practice · 15 min

Teacher: Demonstrate picture-word matching and paired flashcard exchanges.

ICQs: "Say or write?" [Say]. "One or two?" [Two].

Students: Match pictures and practice "I'm a pilot / chef / teacher ..."

Optional game: Find Your Partner (7 min).

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

4. Independent Practice & Communication · 30 min

0–5 min: Flashcard retrieval. Students identify jobs with less teacher support. Optional: Flashcard Challenge.

5–12 min: Listen to the assigned song, then repeat or sing with gestures.

12–22 min: Activity Book matching/tracing. Model one example. ICQ: "Line or color?" [Line].

22–30 min: Job role-play. Students select job cards and say "I'm a ..." Optional: Job Charades.

5. Assessment & Closure · 15 min

0–8 min: Show six job pictures. Record recognition and pronunciation.

8–13 min: Students attempt two "I'm a ..." sentences using different cards. Record independent, prompted, or modeled support.

13–15 min: Praise progress, review a difficult word, and preview next lesson.

Optional game: Pass and Say can replace part of oral assessment practice.

INTEGRATED SUPPORT & FOLLOW-UP

Differentiation

Support: Picture bank, gestures, choral repetition, sentence strips, peer modeling.

Core: Identify jobs and produce supported sentences.

Extension: Independent sentence production and simple job questions.

Evidence & Teacher Reflection

Checklist: Recognition ___/6 · Pronunciation ___/6 · Sentences ___/2.

Support: Independent / Prompted / Modeled.

Reflection: Which words need reteaching? Who needs additional visual support?

IEC Teacher Game & Interactive Activity Guide

Choose a game to replace part of the existing practice time. Demonstrate first. Use short instructions, gestures, pictures, and one-word responses for beginner ELL students.

Period 1 · Warm-up · 5 min

1. Guess the Job

Materials: Job pictures.

  1. Cover most of a job picture.
  2. Reveal one small part.
  3. Students guess by pointing or speaking.
  4. Reveal the answer and repeat together.
ICQ: "Look or write?" [Look].
CCQ: "Who cooks?" [Point to chef].
Check: Correct picture recognition.

Support: Show two choices instead of six.

Period 1 · Modeling · 5 min

2. Listen, Point, Act

Materials: Job cards.

  1. Display two or three job pictures.
  2. Say "Pilot" and model pointing.
  3. Students point to the named job.
  4. Add actions for flying, cooking, dancing.
ICQ: "Point or draw?" [Point].
CCQ: "Who flies?" [Pilot].
Check: Correct pointing before speaking.

Support: Teacher acts first; students copy.

Period 1 · Guided · 7 min

3. Find Your Partner

Materials: Matching pairs of job cards.

  1. Give each student one job card.
  2. Demonstrate finding a matching card.
  3. Students walk to find their partner.
  4. Pairs practice "I'm a doctor."
ICQs: "One or two?" [Two]. "Walk or run?" [Walk].
CCQ: Show two matching cards: "Same?" [Yes].
Check: Matching and sentence attempts.

Support: Use seated pairs if movement is difficult.

Period 2 · Retrieval · 5 min

4. Flashcard Challenge

Materials: Cards or browser game below.

  1. Show one picture.
  2. Students think and name the job.
  3. Invite a sentence: "I'm a ..."
  4. Repeat with the next picture.
ICQ: "Look or write?" [Look].
CCQ: "Who flies?" [Pilot].
Check: Independent vocabulary recall.

Support: Choral responses before individual turns.

Period 2 · Speaking · 8 min

5. Job Charades

Materials: Job cards.

  1. Teacher acts out cooking.
  2. Students guess "Chef."
  3. Students take turns acting out jobs.
  4. Class practices "I'm a chef."
ICQ: "Talk or act?" [Act].
CCQ: "Chef or pilot?" [Chef].
Check: Job meaning and oral production.

Support: Students may act in pairs.

Period 2 · Closure · 5 min

6. Pass and Say

Materials: One soft job picture card.

  1. Students pass a card around the group.
  2. Teacher says "Stop."
  3. Student holding the card names the job.
  4. Student attempts "I'm a ..."
ICQ: "Pass or throw?" [Pass].
CCQ: "Doctor or dancer?" [Doctor].
Check: Independent, prompted, or modeled answer.

Support: Allow pointing or partner help.
