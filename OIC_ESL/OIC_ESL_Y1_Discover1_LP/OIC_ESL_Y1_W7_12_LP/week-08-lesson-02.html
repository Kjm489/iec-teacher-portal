Measurable Learning Objectives

Identify or demonstrate the key ideas in “What can’t you do?” in 3 of 4 supported checks. Use lesson vocabulary in at least two partner or group speaking turns. Complete a topic-related task and share one thing learned.

Teacher QA check: Confirm the named target words, textbook task and OIC codes against the original source. Timings refer to the seven IEC stages (8+12+15+10 / 25+10+10 = 90 minutes); original source activity descriptions may need shortening to fit. Six games are optional alternatives, not six additional required tasks.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Start to chant or sing a line from the chant or song from the previous lesson. Can children continue? Play the chant or song for everyone to join in. Elicit other key words they learned in the previous lesson through mime. Ask children to take some deep breaths with you. Say: Breathe deeply with me. Many people feel calmer after breathing deeply. Today, we’re going to breathe deeply and see how we feel.

TESOL scaffolding: Show the first picture or action; ask students to identify the topic. ICQ: Do we start by watching or speaking? CCQ: Which example matches today’s topic?

Task ICQ: Do you listen and look first, then tell your partner one idea? Lesson CCQ: If we share a ball, do we both get a turn or does one person keep it?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Take three deep breaths together. Using the vocabulary flashcards, elicit: I can run, but I can’t ride a bicycle. Ask children: Can you run? Can you ride a bicycle? Drill the question by using a loud voice and then a very quiet voice for children to copy. Take another three deep breaths together. Place a tick or a cross beside each of the vocabulary flashcards. Children ask the question Can you …? and then answer according to the tick/cross with Yes, I can and No, I can’t. Turn to Oxford Discover SB 1 p.173. Children work in pairs to complete ex.E. Take another three deep breaths together.

TESOL scaffolding: Model a source example aloud with a picture/gesture before learners respond. ICQ: Do you watch my example first? CCQ: Which action or picture matches the model?

Task ICQ: Do you watch and listen to my example before trying it? Lesson CCQ: When friends have fun together, are they enjoying the activity or feeling bored?

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Model a source activity with a volunteer, then repeat with the class. Partners practise the target vocabulary or expression for “What can’t you do?” using visual prompts. Circulate and provide supportive correction; note learners needing extra help.

TESOL scaffolding: Complete one original source exercise together; fade from full prompt to picture cue. ICQ: Do we answer together first? CCQ: Which response fits the example?

Task ICQ: Are we completing the first example together? Lesson CCQ: Can two friends share one game? What would they do?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Model a source activity with a volunteer, then repeat with the class. Partners practise the target vocabulary or expression for “What can’t you do?” using visual prompts. Circulate and provide supportive correction; note learners needing extra help.

TESOL scaffolding: Use pairs to rehearse the source target; provide a sentence frame and swap roles. ICQ: Does each partner take a turn? CCQ: Does the answer match the picture or action?

Task ICQ: Does each partner have a turn to speak or show an answer? Lesson CCQ: If we share a ball, do we both get a turn or does one person keep it?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

On the board, draw a table with six rows and four columns. The left-hand column has five children’s names and the top row has three activities. Do an example with a confident child by asking Can you …? and then ticking or crossing for the activity. Elicit what children can say if they don’t understand (I don’t understand.). Put children into groups of five. Distribute the table handouts, and children complete it with the names of their five group members and three activities. In their groups, children stand up and ask questions, then note down the answers with a tick (can) or a cross (can’t). Take another three deep breaths together. Hold a whole-class feedback session and find out if anyone could do all the activities they were asked about. Choose one or two of the six optional games below; require children to speak about “What can’t you do?” during play.

TESOL scaffolding: Students complete an original source task individually; record who needs prompting. ICQ: Are you working on your own now? CCQ: Can you show the answer without my model?

Task ICQ: Will you complete your own answer before asking for help? Lesson CCQ: When friends have fun together, are they enjoying the activity or feeling bored?

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Support: picture choices, gestures, partner model, short sentence frames. Challenge: ask learners to add a reason, detail or follow-up question.

TESOL scaffolding: Offer picture/word bank for support; require a reason or extra detail for extension. ICQ: Can you use the word bank if needed? CCQ: Can you explain one extra detail?

Task ICQ: Can you use the picture or sentence starter if you need support? Lesson CCQ: Can two friends share one game? What would they do?

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Children have asked and answered questions about what they can and can’t do. Differentiation To support less able children, they can ask only two children, instead of five. Write the questions and answers on the board. Check four learners or groups for understanding; record independent / prompted / not yet demonstrated. Exit task: each learner points, says, draws or demonstrates one target item.

TESOL scaffolding: Use a short independent demonstration or exit response from the source goal. ICQ: Do you show your own answer? CCQ: Which example demonstrates today’s learning?

Task ICQ: Do you show or say one answer without copying a partner? Lesson CCQ: If we share a ball, do we both get a turn or does one person keep it?
