PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Introductory activity Put children into groups. Tell them they are going to make a word with their bodies. Spell out the letters of ‘story’ without saying what the word is. At the end, ask them to remember the order of the letters and to tell you the word. Ask children if they know a story about food. If they do, invite them to tell it. As they do, write on the board ‘beginning’, ‘middle’, and ‘end’, and encourage them to think about the structure of the story in this way. If they don’t know a story about food, prompt them to think of one including food (e.g. Goldilocks eating porridge in ‘Goldilocks and the Three Bears’).

Diagnostic: invite learners to name or point to one example linked to a story about food (1).
