Measurable Learning Objectives

Use the original OIC learning outcomes (1.4b, 1.4d) in the lesson: My favourite clothes (2).

Demonstrate understanding of the lesson vocabulary through the specified original activity.

Produce a supported response and complete an independent check related to my favourite clothes (2).

Teacher QA check: Confirm the named target words, textbook task and OIC codes against the original source. Timings refer to the seven IEC stages (8+12+15+10 / 25+10+10 = 90 minutes); original source activity descriptions may need shortening to fit. Six games are optional alternatives, not six additional required tasks.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Introductory activity Revise clothes vocabulary. Say a word and mime putting on the item. By regularly reviewing recently learned vocabulary, children are helping to build their long-term memory of this learning. Invite a confident child to call out some items and do their own mime. Check that children know the days of the week in English – they will need them for the main activity.

Diagnostic: invite learners to name or point to one example linked to my favourite clothes (2).

Task ICQ: Do you listen and look first, then tell your partner one idea? Lesson CCQ: Using the lesson example for “My favourite clothes (2)”, which picture, action or response shows the target meaning? Explain or demonstrate.

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Model the clothing words with picture cards or real garments. Demonstrate the source task while describing what a person is wearing.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Use the original main activity with teacher prompts: Main activity Show children your pre-prepared paper chain doll. Ask: Who is this? Elicit that it’s you, then ask: Has anyone made a paper chain doll? Explain that today’s objective is to make a paper chain doll to represent children on each week day, then describe its clothes. In unison, make a paper chain with (five) dolls. If you decide to use a template, this will be much easier as children will only need to cut, rather than draw and cut. If anyone inadvertently cuts in the wrong place, let them stick their dolls onto a piece of paper. When all the dolls have been cut out, challenge children to think about what they need to do and to break down their task into sensible steps. 4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partners take turns completing a new example from the source activity. One responds and the other checks, then they switch. ICQs: Do you choose the clothing picture first or say the sentence first? Will your partner describe or point? Demonstrate one turn.

Sentence frames: I am wearing ___. / He or she is wearing ___. / It is ___.

Task ICQ: Does each partner have a turn to speak or show an answer? Lesson CCQ: Using the lesson example for “My favourite clothes (2)”, which picture, action or response shows the target meaning? Explain or demonstrate.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Additional tasks Complete Oxford Discover WB 1 p.35 exs.A–B. Draw a family member in their favourite clothes, perhaps at a party or a festival.

Gradually remove the picture/word prompt. Assess the learner’s independent response using: I am wearing ___. / He or she is wearing ___. / It is ___.

Task ICQ: Will you complete your own answer before asking for help? Lesson CCQ: Using the lesson example for “My favourite clothes (2)”, which picture, action or response shows the target meaning? Explain or demonstrate.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Differentiation To support less able children, give them a template to cut out. You could also partially cut it out for them. Reduce the number of dolls for less confident children (e.g. weekend clothes for Friday, Saturday, and Sunday). As an extension task for more able children, ask them to draw and cut their doll out independently, but monitor them actively.

Fade support from teacher model → picture cue → sentence starter → independent explanation.

Task ICQ: Can you use the picture or sentence starter if you need support? Lesson CCQ: Using the lesson example for “My favourite clothes (2)”, which picture, action or response shows the target meaning? Explain or demonstrate.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Learning review Children have created a paper chain doll and illustrated it with different outfits, thinking carefully about each step of the process. They have described their paper chain doll in simple sentences, using a range of topic vocabulary.

Exit ticket: Choose a new outfit picture, name two items and say one correct sentence.

Teacher records who can respond independently, with prompts, or needs reteaching.

Task ICQ: Do you show or say one answer without copying a partner? Lesson CCQ: Using the lesson example for “My favourite clothes (2)”, which picture, action or response shows the target meaning? Explain or demonstrate.
