4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partners take turns completing a new example from the source activity. One responds and the other checks, then they switch. ICQs: Do you choose the clothing picture first or say the sentence first? Will your partner describe or point? Demonstrate one turn.

Sentence frames: I am wearing ___. / He or she is wearing ___. / It is ___.

Task ICQ: Does each partner have a turn to speak or show an answer? Lesson CCQ: In the lesson example, what does “Clothes I wear” mean? Point to or explain the matching picture/action.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Individually complete a new version of the original task for session 1 of 5.

Gradually remove the picture/word prompt. Assess the learner’s independent response using: I am wearing ___. / He or she is wearing ___. / It is ___.

Task ICQ: Will you complete your own answer before asking for help? Lesson CCQ: In the lesson example, what does “Clothes I wear” mean? Point to or explain the matching picture/action.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Differentiation Encourage children who need more support to answer simple questions with tick/cross cards. Provide language and gapped texts, and put them into groups with more able children. For more able children, encourage them to do more. For example, they could write questions and true or false statements for the race track game.

Fade support from teacher model → picture cue → sentence starter → independent explanation.

Task ICQ: Can you use the picture or sentence starter if you need support? Lesson CCQ: In the lesson example, what does “Clothes I wear” mean? Point to or explain the matching picture/action.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Learning review Children have consolidated their knowledge and learning on the topic of ‘Clothes I wear’.

Exit ticket: Complete one unfamiliar review prompt independently and explain how you checked the answer.

Teacher records who can respond independently, with prompts, or needs reteaching.

Task ICQ: Do you show or say one answer without copying a partner? Lesson CCQ: In the lesson example, what does “Clothes I wear” mean? Point to or explain the matching picture/action.
