5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Additional tasks Role-play the dialogues in Oxford Discover WB 1 p.89 ex.A. Create an extra verse for the song or chant.

Gradually remove the picture/word prompt. Assess the learner’s independent response using: This is ___. / I eat ___. / It comes from ___.

Task ICQ: Will you complete your own answer before asking for help? Lesson CCQ: In the lesson example, what does “like” mean? Point to or explain the matching picture/action.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Differentiation As an extension task for more able children, ask them to think of food combinations. Ask, for example: Do you like sausage sandwiches? 7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Learning review Children have consolidated their understanding of food vocabulary by learning a song/chant about food. They have asked and answered questions about their food preferences and carried out a whole-class survey.

Exit ticket: Identify one food or drink from a new picture and explain one fact about it.

Teacher records who can respond independently, with prompts, or needs reteaching.

Task ICQ: Do you show or say one answer without copying a partner? Lesson CCQ: In the lesson example, what does “flashing” mean? Point to or explain the matching picture/action.

Original OIC context Context
This is the second lesson of the unit ‘Food and drink’.
In this lesson, children will develop their speaking skills by carrying out a survey to find out about foods they like and foods they don’t like. 
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.

Elicit from children that they need to think of small steps to get the big answers. You could suggest that they think of three things to do (e.g. think of questions, write questions, draw a tick/cross as an answer). Put children into small groups to think of questions and a way to record their answers (e.g. tick/cross, happy/sad face, colour coded, a tally, etc.).
