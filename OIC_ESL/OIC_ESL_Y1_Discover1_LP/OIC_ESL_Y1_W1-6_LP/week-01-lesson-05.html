LESSON INFORMATION & OBJECTIVES
Curriculum: OIC ESL Year 1
Theme: Me and my classroom
Week / Lesson: 1 / 5
Topic: School and classroom (2)
Duration: 90 minutes (45 + 45)
OIC Outcomes: 1.1e, 1.4c

Learning Objectives & Success Criteria

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Recognize and name six common school supplies.
  2. Identify objects through listening and real-object demonstrations.
  3. Match picture cards with corresponding word cards.
  4. Copy, illustrate and use school-supply vocabulary appropriately.

Success: Students recognize at least four of the six school-supply words, match objects to pictures or written words and complete a supported writing/drawing task.

Target Language, Materials & Preparation

Original vocabulary: backpack, eraser, notebook, pen, pencil, ruler.

Language: What's in the backpack? It's a ___. Show me the ___. I have a ___.

Materials: Backpack containing the six target items, real school supplies, picture flashcards, word cards, mini cards, notebooks and crayons.

Textbook: Oxford Discover Student Book 1 p. 94, Exercise A; Workbook 1 p. 80, Exercises A and C.

Preparation: Place target items in the backpack. Prepare matching picture and word cards and student notebooks.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge — 8 minutes

Teacher: Show a closed backpack. Ask, "What's in my backpack?" Invite predictions without opening it.

Reveal one item at a time. Students observe, listen and repeat the name of each object.

Students: Predict contents, identify familiar items and repeat target vocabulary.

Scaffolding: Show real objects and allow pointing before spoken responses.

ICQ: "Do we guess before I open the backpack?" Yes.

CCQ: Hold up a pencil. "Is this a pencil or a ruler?" Pencil.

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO — 12 minutes

Teacher: Display each real item and its corresponding picture and word card. Say the word, demonstrate pronunciation and model matching the three representations.

Use Oxford Discover Student Book 1 p. 94, Exercise A, as the textbook vocabulary reference.

Teacher: "What's this?" (Holds up a ruler.)
Teacher: "It's a ruler."
Teacher: "Show me the ruler." (Points to ruler.)

Model writing one word beneath its picture in a notebook. Demonstrate the difference between a pencil and pen using the real objects.

ICQ: "Do you watch how I match the object, picture and word first?" Yes.

CCQs: "Do we use a ruler to measure or erase?" Measure. "Does an eraser remove pencil marks?" Yes.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO — 15 minutes

Activity A — Show me: Say "Show me the notebook" or another target item. Students point to or hold up the correct object.

Activity B — Picture and word matching: Display picture cards and word cards. Complete the first match together, then invite students to match the remaining cards with teacher guidance.

Activity C — Memory challenge: Show several picture cards, turn them face down and invite students to locate matching picture/word pairs.

ICQs: "When I say 'Show me the ruler,' do you point to the ruler?" Yes. "Do we match the word to the correct picture?" Yes.

CCQs: "Is a notebook used for writing?" Yes. "Is a backpack used to carry school things?" Yes.

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice — 10 minutes

Teacher: Distribute mini picture and word cards. Model a memory matching round with a volunteer. Students take turns turning over cards and identifying matches.

Partners practise "What's this?" / "It's a ___" using real objects and picture cards.

Scaffolding: Begin with three pairs of cards. Increase to six pairs for students who are ready.

ICQs: "Do you turn over two cards?" Yes. "If they match, do you keep the pair?" Yes.

CCQ: "Does the word 'eraser' match a picture of a pen?" No.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO — 25 minutes

Activity A — My school supplies (10 min): Students draw selected school supplies in their notebooks, copy the corresponding vocabulary words and check their drawings against the real objects.

Activity B — Independent matching (7 min): Students match picture and word cards or complete the appropriate Oxford Discover Workbook 1 p. 80 activities.

Activity C — Color dictation and identification (8 min): Students follow instructions such as "Color the ruler blue" and identify the named school supplies independently.

ICQs: "Do you draw the object and copy its word?" Yes. "Do you color the object I name?" Yes.

CCQs: "If the word is 'pen,' should you draw a ruler?" No. "Can you use a pencil to write in your notebook?" Yes.

Assessment evidence: Drawings, copied words, independent matches and responses to object instructions.

6. Differentiation & Scaffolding — 10 minutes

Support:

  • Begin with four target words.
  • Use real objects before picture cards.
  • Provide picture-word models.
  • Offer dotted or partially written words.
  • Allow oral identification before copying.

Extension:

  • Match all six words without prompts.
  • Write additional object labels.
  • Describe the contents of a backpack.
  • Make labels for personal school supplies.
  • Ask a partner questions independently.

ICQ: "Can you use a picture-word model if you need help?" Yes.

CCQ: "Is a notebook the same as a backpack?" No.

7. Formative Assessment, Review & Exit Ticket — 10 minutes

Review: Display or remove objects from the backpack. Ask students to name the items, point to matching pictures and identify written words.

Exit ticket: Each student:

  1. Identifies two school supplies from real objects.
  2. Matches one word card to its picture.
  3. Shows a drawing and copied word from their notebook.

Final CCQs: "Do we use an eraser to remove pencil marks?" Yes. "Do we use a ruler to carry books?" No.

Teacher records: Independent / With prompt / Not yet demonstrated.

TEACHER REFLECTION & NEXT STEPS

Post-Lesson Evaluation

Vocabulary: Which school-supply words can students recognize independently?

Literacy: Which students can match, copy and illustrate the target words?

Listening: Can students follow "Show me" and color dictation instructions?

Next steps: Revisit insecure vocabulary and build on classroom and school-supply language in Week 2.
