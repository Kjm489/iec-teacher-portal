LESSON INFORMATION & OBJECTIVES
Curriculum: OIC ESL Year 1
Theme: Me and my classroom
Week / Lesson: 1 / 4
Topic: School and classroom (1)
Duration: 90 minutes (45 + 45)
OIC Outcomes: 1.1e, 1.2a

Learning Objectives & Success Criteria

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Recognize and name common classroom objects.
  2. Match classroom vocabulary with pictures and real objects.
  3. Respond to simple instructions involving classroom objects and colors.
  4. Participate in flashcard, missing-card and matching games.

Success: Students identify at least six target classroom objects, respond to simple object instructions and demonstrate vocabulary understanding during games.

Target Language, Materials & Preparation

Original vocabulary: bag, bin, board, book, chair, cupboard, desk, door, poster, window.

Language: What's this? It's a ___. Show me the ___. I can see a ___. How many ___?

Materials: Classroom flashcards, mini flashcards, scissors, crayons, matching cards and real classroom objects.

Textbook: Oxford Discover Student Book 1 p. 28, Exercises A–B.

Preparation: Prepare ten picture flashcards and mini card sets. Organize space for Snap! and missing-card games.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge — 8 minutes

Teacher: Greet students and invite them to look around the classroom. Point to familiar objects and ask "What's this?" Accept pointing or known words before modeling new vocabulary.

Review counting from Lesson 3 by counting chairs, desks or windows together.

Students: Look, point, count and name familiar classroom objects.

ICQ: "Do we look around the classroom or outside the school?" Around the classroom.

CCQ: Point to a window. "Is this a window or a door?" Window.

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO — 12 minutes

Teacher: Display each of the ten original OIC vocabulary flashcards. Say the word, point to the corresponding classroom object when possible and model pronunciation.

Demonstrate using Oxford Discover Student Book 1 p. 28, Exercises A–B, to connect pictures with classroom words.

Teacher: "What's this?" (Points to a chair.)
Teacher: "It's a chair."
Teacher: "Show me the door." (Points to door.)

Model the missing-card game: display four cards, ask students to look, hide one card and identify which is missing.

ICQ: "Do you look at all the cards before one disappears?" Yes.

CCQs: "Do we sit on a chair or a window?" Chair. "Do we usually write on the board or the door?" Board.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO — 15 minutes

Activity A — Flashcard recognition: Show classroom flashcards. Students repeat the words, point to matching objects and identify cards without spoken models.

Activity B — What's missing?: Display four to six flashcards, hide one and ask the class to identify the missing object.

Activity C — Color dictation: Give simple instructions using pictures and colors, such as "Color the chair blue." Students demonstrate understanding by coloring the correct object.

ICQs: "When a card disappears, do you name the missing card?" Yes. "Do you color the object I name?" Yes.

CCQs: "Is a cupboard used for storage?" Yes. "Is a bin used for rubbish?" Yes.

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice — 10 minutes

Teacher: Give pairs mini flashcards. Student A arranges four cards; Student B looks away while one is removed. Student B identifies the missing card. Students swap roles.

Then demonstrate Snap!: students compare picture cards and call "Snap!" when matching pictures appear.

Scaffolding: Start with four picture cards, then increase the number. Keep a vocabulary word bank visible.

ICQs: "Do you look away when your partner hides a card?" Yes. "Do you call Snap when the pictures match?" Yes.

CCQ: "If both cards show a book, do they match?" Yes.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO — 25 minutes

Activity A — Classroom object hunt (8 min): Students locate named classroom objects independently and identify them orally or by pointing.

Activity B — Mini flashcard challenge (8 min): Students independently match classroom pictures to words or real objects, using their mini flashcards.

Activity C — Color dictation and Snap! (9 min): Students complete short object/color instructions, then practise matching cards with a partner using minimal teacher prompting.

ICQs: "Do you find the object I name?" Yes. "Do you match the same objects?" Yes.

CCQs: "Is a poster usually displayed on a wall?" Yes. "Is a desk the same as a chair?" No.

Assessment evidence: Record accurate vocabulary recognition, picture matching and responses to instructions.

6. Differentiation & Scaffolding — 10 minutes

Support:

  • Begin with four to six target words.
  • Use picture and real-object matching.
  • Provide choral repetition.
  • Keep vocabulary cards visible.
  • Use two-choice identification questions.

Extension:

  • Match written words to pictures.
  • Count classroom objects.
  • Give a partner simple instructions.
  • Describe an object using color.
  • Complete matching without visual prompts.

ICQ: "Can you use the picture cards if you need help?" Yes.

CCQ: "Does a window let us look outside?" Yes.

7. Formative Assessment, Review & Exit Ticket — 10 minutes

Review: Display the ten classroom flashcards in mixed order. Ask students to identify selected pictures and point to the matching real objects.

Exit ticket: Each student:

  1. Names or identifies three classroom objects.
  2. Matches one picture to a real classroom object.
  3. Follows one simple classroom-object instruction.

Final CCQs: "Do we open a door to enter a room?" Yes. "Is a bin the same as a cupboard?" No.

Teacher records: Independent / With prompt / Not yet demonstrated.

TEACHER REFLECTION & NEXT STEPS

Post-Lesson Evaluation

Vocabulary: Which classroom words are secure? Which require reteaching?

Listening: Can students follow color and object instructions?

Scaffolding: Did real objects or picture flashcards provide better support?
