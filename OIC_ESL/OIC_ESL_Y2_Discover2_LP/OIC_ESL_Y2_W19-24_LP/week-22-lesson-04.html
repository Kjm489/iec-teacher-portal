Measurable Learning Objectives

Lesson: 4

Measurable targets: Identify key ideas about reducing waste and reusing materials; practise reduce / reuse / save; independently identify actions that reduce or reuse. Check understanding through a relevant oral or written response.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Teacher: Put children into small groups and tell them they are going to spell out a word with their bodies. Spell out (letter by letter) ‘Earth’. Children sit down, remember the letters in order, and tell you the word. It is a tricky spelling, so say the word carefully with them, paying attention to the final soft ‘th’ sound. Ask children if our planet is important and elicit some reasons why. Explain that today’s lesson focuses on looking after our planet, Earth.

Student action: Think, pair, share a lesson-related example before viewing the model. ICQs: “Are we sharing what we know or writing our final answer?” “Who speaks first?” CCQs: “Which action, picture or word connects to reducing waste and reusing materials?”

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner exchange: In pairs, learners practise reduce / reuse / save through reducing waste and reusing materials prompts. Partner A gives a description, question or example; Partner B responds and points to evidence. Swap roles and repeat with a different prompt.

ICQs: “Do both partners have a turn?” “When do you switch?” CCQs: “Was the response appropriate for this situation?”

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application: Complete ex.B in Oxford Discover SB 2 p.108 and exs.A–D in WB 2 pp.92–93. Compete a KWL chart for the Big Question. Find pictures of natural resources online. Make a list of natural resources and what humankind is doing to destroy them, e.g. for water: throwing rubbish in rivers, seas, and lakes.

Students complete their own response, drawing, map, chart, writing, dialogue or project artifact as the original lesson requires. Teacher checks whether each learner can independently identify actions that reduce or reuse without copying the group model.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: picture/word cards, gesture, two-choice questions and a sentence starter using reduce / reuse / save. Developing: complete a full response with one relevant detail. Extending: explain why, compare two examples, or create a new scenario.

Original differentiation: To support less able children, provide some ideas for different ways to look after the Earth. Ask them to decide which one is the most important. Encourage more able children to set several goals to reduce and reuse.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Original review aim: Children have thought about how we can take care of the Earth and the natural world. They have understood what it means to ‘reduce’ and ‘reuse’. They have practised using ‘going to’ to express an intention.

Exit ticket: Respond to a fresh prompt about reducing waste and reusing materials using reduce / reuse / save. Mark Independent / Prompted / Not yet and identify who needs follow-up practice. CCQ: “What did we learn and how do we show it?”
