4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner exchange: In pairs, learners practise unit vocabulary and complete sentences through Present a community care guide or recycling report prompts. Partner A gives a description, question or example; Partner B responds and points to evidence. Swap roles and repeat with a different prompt.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application: Use the source review activity choices to complete: Present a community care guide or recycling report.

Students complete their own response, drawing, map, chart, writing, dialogue or project artifact as the original lesson requires. Teacher checks whether each learner can independently Present a community care guide or recycling report without copying the group model.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: picture/word cards, gesture, two-choice questions and a sentence starter using unit vocabulary and complete sentences. Developing: complete a full response with one relevant detail. Extending: explain why, compare two examples, or create a new scenario.

Original differentiation: For children who need more support provide sentences, questions, and words, either on the board or in gapped texts. Pair them with more able children. They may look back at their work to help them, or produce less. Give them choices: e.g. ask: Would you prefer to speak or write for this activity? Challenge more able children to write and say more sentences, providing additional details and using new language and grammatical forms learned.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Original review aim: Children have consolidated their knowledge and learning on the topic of ‘Looking after our places’.

Exit ticket: Respond to a fresh prompt about Present a community care guide or recycling report using unit vocabulary and complete sentences. Mark Independent / Prompted / Not yet and identify who needs follow-up practice. CCQ: “What did we learn and how do we show it?”

WE DO — guided example: Complete a second example together. Ask students to identify the correct source clue, then elicit a full sentence about asking permission and sharing.

YOU DO — game procedure: One learner silently acts out a source action; partners identify it and say a complete sentence.

Support: Show two visual choices; rehearse the frame “May I borrow your ___? Yes, you may. / Sorry, I need it.” before learners respond.

Challenge: Ask for a second example or an explanation using because, while remaining on the topic of asking permission and sharing.
