5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application: Use the source review activity choices to complete: Write and rehearse a good-manners role-play.

Students complete their own response, drawing, map, chart, writing, dialogue or project artifact as the original lesson requires. Teacher checks whether each learner can independently Write and rehearse a good-manners role-play without copying the group model.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: picture/word cards, gesture, two-choice questions and a sentence starter using unit vocabulary and complete sentences. Developing: complete a full response with one relevant detail. Extending: explain why, compare two examples, or create a new scenario.

Original differentiation: For children who need more support provide sentences, questions, and words, either on the board or in gapped texts. Pair them with more able children. They may look back at their work to help them, or produce less. Give them choices: e.g. ask: Would you prefer to speak or write for this activity? Challenge more able children to write and say more sentences, providing additional details and using new language and grammatical forms learned.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Original review aim: Children have consolidated their knowledge and learning on the topic of ‘Thinking of others’.
