Measurable Learning Objectives

Lesson: 4

Measurable targets: Identify key ideas about sorting and recycling materials; practise paper / plastic / glass / recycle; independently categorize materials and explain recycling. Check understanding through a relevant oral or written response.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Teacher: Ask children to name all the natural resources they can remember. Take answers and write them on the board. Elicit how these can be ruined, and what we can do (reduce, reuse, recycle, etc.). Tell the class that today they are going to read a story about recycling. Show your recycled objects and invite children to guess what they are made from. Then ask them to tell you about any recycled object they have and what it is made from.

Student action: Think, pair, share a lesson-related example before viewing the model. ICQs: “Are we sharing what we know or writing our final answer?” “Who speaks first?” CCQs: “Which action, picture or word connects to sorting and recycling materials?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher demonstration: Explicitly model the first step of the original task: Turn to Oxford Discover SB 2 pp.118–119. Tell children to look at the pictures. Ask: What can you see? What do you think the story is about? In pairs, children use their imagination to come up with a story based on the pictures. Remind them that something must happen in the story. Read the story as a class, inviting children to read a few sentences each, if possible. Compare their initial story ideas to what really happened. Ask: How is it similar? How is it different? Focus on the tenses used in the story. Look at the Writing Study in Oxford Discover SB 2 p.123. Tell children to read the story again and to underline/note down examples of these tenses in different colours. Form small groups. Explain to children that they are going to test each other’s understanding of the story by making questions in their group for another group to answer. 4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner exchange: In pairs, learners practise paper / plastic / glass / recycle through sorting and recycling materials prompts. Partner A gives a description, question or example; Partner B responds and points to evidence. Swap roles and repeat with a different prompt.

ICQs: “Do both partners have a turn?” “When do you switch?” CCQs: “Was the response appropriate for this situation?”

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application: Complete exs.A–C in Oxford Discover SB 2 p.120. Create a chart or diagram of objects that can be recycled and what they can become.

Students complete their own response, drawing, map, chart, writing, dialogue or project artifact as the original lesson requires. Teacher checks whether each learner can independently categorize materials and explain recycling without copying the group model.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: picture/word cards, gesture, two-choice questions and a sentence starter using paper / plastic / glass / recycle. Developing: complete a full response with one relevant detail. Extending: explain why, compare two examples, or create a new scenario.

Original differentiation: To support less able children, give them some question starters or whole questions but with the words in the wrong order. As an extension task for more able children, ask them to think of different types of question, such as multiple choice or true or false statements.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Original review aim: Children have read about how an object can be recycled.

Exit ticket: Respond to a fresh prompt about sorting and recycling materials using paper / plastic / glass / recycle. Mark Independent / Prompted / Not yet and identify who needs follow-up practice. CCQ: “What did we learn and how do we show it?”
