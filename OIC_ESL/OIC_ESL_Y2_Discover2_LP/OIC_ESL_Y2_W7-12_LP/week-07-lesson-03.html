Measurable Learning Objectives

Lesson: 3

Measurable success: Learners complete the original singing about the past task and communicate one accurate response with appropriate evidence.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Activate: Play a game. Tell children you are going to say some sentences. If it’s something that happens now (present), they hop three times. If it’s something that happened in the past, but doesn’t happen now, they sit down: e.g. People used candles to see in the dark. People fly to the moon. Ask children to think about life in the past. Do they think it was easier or more difficult than now? Encourage them to share any stories from their grandparents, and to listen carefully to others’ perspectives.

ICQs: “Are we sharing ideas first or writing a final answer?” “Who speaks first?” CCQs: “What is the lesson about?” “Which example belongs to Past and present?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher models the original task: Turn to Oxford Discover SB 2 p.53. Introduce the context – ‘Great Grandma travelled on a ship’ – and elicit some things she did (move/listen to news/walk). Write four regular past tense verbs on the board. Tell children they are going to listen to a song. They listen and put the verbs in order. Play the song again. This time, children stand up when they hear the word. Write a sentence from your song with a verb on the board: e.g. ‘Grandma travelled on a ship.’ Ask: When did she travel? Elicit ‘in the past’ or ‘40 years ago’, etc. Ask: How do we know it’s in the past? Tell children to look at ‘travelled’ and elicit that when an action word ends in ‘–ed’, it’s the past. Sing the song with children. Ask children how they feel after singing the song together as a group. Encourage children to think about life in the past. Ask some questions, then ask children to think of some sentences: e.g. People walked everywhere. People used candles at night. Provide a word bank on the board to help them. In pairs, children think of things they did last night. Individually, they each choose one sentence and mime it for their group to guess what they did.

Think aloud to show how to identify evidence, pronounce key words and produce a complete answer. Contrast one correct and one incorrect example. CCQs: “What tells us this answer is correct?” “Which detail in the source supports it?”

Use visuals, chunk instructions, give response options, then fade the model. ICQs: “Do we work together first?” “Where do we find the answer?” CCQs: “How can we check our response?”

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner practice: Pupils take turns explaining or questioning each other about singing about the past using the original lesson materials. Partners identify a detail from the task, explain it, then switch roles.

ICQs: “Do both partners speak?” “Do you change roles?” CCQs: “Does your answer agree with the picture, text or example?”

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent task: Learners independently complete an age-appropriate product from the original task: Complete Oxford Discover WB 2 pp.42–43. Write sentences in the past. Say: This is what you did yesterday. Children write sentences with ‘play’, ‘jump’, and ‘walk’. Play the game ‘Bingo’ with regular past tense verbs. Work together to make a ‘This is what I did yesterday’ book.

Collect an oral answer, short written response, labeled picture, comparison, sequence, or review project as appropriate to the original learning outcome.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: visual choices, word bank, teacher sentence model and rehearsal. Developing: complete response with a supporting detail. Extending: add a reason, comparison or follow-up question.

Original differentiation: To support less able children, pair them with more able children so peer support can be provided. Challenge more confident children to create a new verse for the song.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Review original goal: Children have sung a song in the past tense and have learned how to distinguish between regular past and present tenses.

Exit ticket: Respond independently to a new question or visual directly related to “Singing about the past”. Mark independent / prompted / not yet. CCQ: “What have we learned about this topic?”
