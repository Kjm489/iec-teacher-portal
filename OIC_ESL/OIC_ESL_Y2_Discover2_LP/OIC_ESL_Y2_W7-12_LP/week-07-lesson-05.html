Measurable Learning Objectives

Lesson: 5

Measurable success: Learners complete the original news time! task and communicate one accurate response with appropriate evidence.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Activate: Tell children you are going to say three things you did last weekend. Two are true and one isn’t. They need to identify the one that isn’t by asking questions using the past. Tell children that today they are going to work in groups. Ask them to think of some good things about working in a group or team: e.g. You don’t have to think of all the ideas yourself! Give children about four minutes to come up with some ideas. Tell them to remember these, as today they are going to work in a group.

ICQs: “Are we sharing ideas first or writing a final answer?” “Who speaks first?” CCQs: “What is the lesson about?” “Which example belongs to Past and present?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher models the original task: Show children the cardboard box cut-out and elicit that it’s a TV. Tell children they are going to present a TV news item about something that happened. They can invent it, or it can be true. It could also be a news item about the texts from earlier in the week: e.g. ‘People travelled by boat, but now we travel by plane.’ Each person in the group has to say at least one sentence. Encourage the first person to open with ‘Here is the news!’ or something similar. Put children into small groups and hand out a mind map template for them to note down their ideas. Remind children to share their ideas in turn and listen to each other attentively. Encourage them to include at least one idea from everyone in the group. Now children need to write out their segment. Set a time limit (e.g. ten minutes). Monitor actively, noting down areas to work on in future lessons. Give groups time to rehearse their news item. Each group presents their news item to the rest of the class. If possible, film it (if you have permission from their parents). This is a valuable learning tool, as children can see their performance, and later will be able to see their progress.

Think aloud to show how to identify evidence, pronounce key words and produce a complete answer. Contrast one correct and one incorrect example. CCQs: “What tells us this answer is correct?” “Which detail in the source supports it?”

Use visuals, chunk instructions, give response options, then fade the model. ICQs: “Do we work together first?” “Where do we find the answer?” CCQs: “How can we check our response?”

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner practice: Pupils take turns explaining or questioning each other about news time! using the original lesson materials. Partners identify a detail from the task, explain it, then switch roles.

ICQs: “Do both partners speak?” “Do you change roles?” CCQs: “Does your answer agree with the picture, text or example?”

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent task: Learners independently complete an age-appropriate product from the original task: Complete the exercises in Oxford Discover SB 2 p.52. Play some word games with some of the words that came up in the main activity: e.g. ‘Tic-tac-toe’, a spelling dictation, or ‘Snowman’.

Collect an oral answer, short written response, labeled picture, comparison, sequence, or review project as appropriate to the original learning outcome.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: visual choices, word bank, teacher sentence model and rehearsal. Developing: complete response with a supporting detail. Extending: add a reason, comparison or follow-up question.

Original differentiation: To support less able children, give them some topics to choose from. Allow them to write their news item about what they did yesterday. Encourage more able children to guide less able children through the group activity, ensuring that they understand the task and can contribute in a meaningful way. As an extension task for more able children, encourage them to add more detail to their news items.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Review original goal: Children have presented a news item (real or made-up) using past tense verbs.

Exit ticket: Respond independently to a new question or visual directly related to “News time!”. Mark independent / prompted / not yet. CCQ: “What have we learned about this topic?”
