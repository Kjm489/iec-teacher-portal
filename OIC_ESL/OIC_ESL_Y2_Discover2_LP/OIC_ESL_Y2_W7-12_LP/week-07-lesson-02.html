Measurable Learning Objectives

Lesson: 2

Measurable success: Learners complete the original then and now task and communicate one accurate response with appropriate evidence.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Activate: Tell children you’re going to show them a picture very quickly. Ask: Are you ready? Flash the picture of your neighbourhood. Ask children if they saw anything. Show it again, letting them look at it for one second, then two seconds, etc. Elicit anything they saw and write ideas on the board. Carry on until most things from the picture have been elicited. Show the picture to children and say: This is our neighbourhood. What is the name of our neighbourhood? Some children might not live in the school’s neighbourhood. If not, elicit the name of their residential neighbourhood, town, or local area.

ICQs: “Are we sharing ideas first or writing a final answer?” “Who speaks first?” CCQs: “What is the lesson about?” “Which example belongs to Past and present?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher models the original task: Ask children to say or sing the alphabet with you. Spell a word (e.g. ‘quiet’) and ask children to write it down. Check they have understood hearing, saying, and writing their letters. Remind children that everyone makes mistakes, and that it is good to share and learn from them. Tell children that they are going to do a running dictation. Say: Around the room, there are some words. You are going to work in pairs. One person will run to the word, read it, remember how to spell it, and then run back to their partner and say/spell the word. Their partner will write the word down. Then you swap. You should mime all of these actions as you are saying them. Then do an example with a confident child. Put children into pairs. Tell children there are six words to find (see gap-fill words in ‘Additional information’). Start the activity and monitor to make sure children are going to the word, memorizing it, and returning to relay the information to their partner. When everyone has finished, ask six children to write one word each up on the board. Check the meaning of the words by asking for a sentence using each word. Children can do this orally. 4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner practice: Pupils take turns explaining or questioning each other about then and now using the original lesson materials. Partners identify a detail from the task, explain it, then switch roles.

ICQs: “Do both partners speak?” “Do you change roles?” CCQs: “Does your answer agree with the picture, text or example?”

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent task: Learners independently complete an age-appropriate product from the original task: Complete the exercises in Oxford Discover WB 2 pp.38–39. Find texts in a non-fiction book and identify the main idea and the details. Play some spelling games with children: e.g. say a word, go round the class, and each child says one letter. Play ‘Snowman’ (see Week 5, Lesson 1 for instructions).

Collect an oral answer, short written response, labeled picture, comparison, sequence, or review project as appropriate to the original learning outcome.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: visual choices, word bank, teacher sentence model and rehearsal. Developing: complete response with a supporting detail. Extending: add a reason, comparison or follow-up question.

Original differentiation: To support less able children, provide the words, and they have to check the letters that their partner says. As an extension task for more able children, you could put short sentences up around the room, rather than just individual words. Challenge more able children by asking them to write about a favourite topic using the main idea and details structure.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Review original goal: Children have revised the alphabet, and read a text identifying the main idea and details.

Exit ticket: Respond independently to a new question or visual directly related to “Then and now”. Mark independent / prompted / not yet. CCQ: “What have we learned about this topic?”

Context
This is the second lesson of the unit ‘Past and present’.
In this lesson, children will develop their listening and reading skills by participating in a reading relay.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.

If not, elicit the name of their residential neighbourhood, town, or local area.

Depending on time, this could be a group activity. Hand out the gapped text and ask children to write the words in the correct place. Hold a whole-class feedback session and check answers. Write two headings on the board: ‘Main idea’ and ‘Details’. Ask children to tell you which sentence is the ‘main idea’ (the first sentence) and which are the ‘details’ (the other sentences).
