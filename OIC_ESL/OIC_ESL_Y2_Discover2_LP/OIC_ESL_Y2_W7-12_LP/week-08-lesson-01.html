PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Activate: Introduce children to the game ‘Shark fin breathing’. Together with children, place the side of your hand on your forehead, palm facing to the side. Close your eyes and slide your hand down your face to your nose. Say Shhhh as you do this. Instruct children to do the five ‘S’s as they do this: ‘Sit up straight’, ‘Sit still’, ‘Sit silently’, ‘Soft breathing’, and ‘Shut eyes’.

ICQs: “Are we sharing ideas first or writing a final answer?” “Who speaks first?” CCQs: “What is the lesson about?” “Which example belongs to Past and present?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher models the original task: Using the flashcards or pictures, teach the class transport vocabulary. You can play some vocabulary games, such as miming travelling by a form of transport and asking children to call out what it is. Ask children if they can think of a way of grouping the words: e.g. transport with/without wheels. Elicit as many different ways as you can, then ask them to choose one criteria. Show children a Venn diagram and how to complete it. They are going to make their own. Put children into pairs and ask them to work together to place the words in their own Venn diagram, considering the various criteria discussed. Hold a whole-class feedback session asking which criteria worked best. Ask children: Did you travel by boat last week? Elicit Yes, I did. or No, I didn’t. Continue with other forms of transport. 4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner practice: Pupils take turns explaining or questioning each other about travel using the original lesson materials. Partners identify a detail from the task, explain it, then switch roles.

ICQs: “Do both partners speak?” “Do you change roles?” CCQs: “Does your answer agree with the picture, text or example?”

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent task: Learners independently complete an age-appropriate product from the original task: Complete Oxford Discover SB 2 p.54 exs.B–D and WB 2 p.44. Play the game ‘Three things I did last night’. However, change it to ‘Three things I didn’t do last night’. Two statements are true and one is false. Create another Venn diagram about another vocabulary set.

Collect an oral answer, short written response, labeled picture, comparison, sequence, or review project as appropriate to the original learning outcome.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: visual choices, word bank, teacher sentence model and rehearsal. Developing: complete response with a supporting detail. Extending: add a reason, comparison or follow-up question.

Original differentiation: To support less able children, pair them with a more confident child. Give them a character with some transport ideas for that character. As an extension task for more able children, ask them to add more transport items.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Review original goal: Children have learned the negative and interrogative forms of the past simple, learned new transport words, and presented data in a Venn diagram.

Exit ticket: Respond independently to a new question or visual directly related to “Travel”. Mark independent / prompted / not yet. CCQ: “What have we learned about this topic?”

Context
This is the sixth lesson of the unit ‘Past and present’.
In this lesson, children will develop their listening and speaking skills as they learn new topic vocabulary and work together to present data in a Venn diagram.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
