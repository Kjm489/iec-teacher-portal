Measurable Learning Objectives

Lesson: 1

Measurable success: Learners complete the original a healthy body task and communicate one accurate response with appropriate evidence.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Activate: Play a game with children. Say a body part, and they tell you how many they have. Begin with easy words like ‘leg’ or ‘arm’. Then progress to more difficult words such as ‘nails’ or even ‘fingernails’. Ask children if they have strong bodies, and how they know they do (they can move and pick things up, etc.).

ICQs: “Are we sharing ideas first or writing a final answer?” “Who speaks first?” CCQs: “What is the lesson about?” “Which example belongs to Keeping healthy?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher models the original task: Elicit the following words: ‘healthy skin’, ‘good teeth’, ‘strong bones’, and ‘shiny hair’. Ask children if they have these, and if they know what they need to do to keep them that way. Using flashcards, elicit the names of punctuation marks and when we use them. On the board, write two sentences from the text without punctuation, and ask children to copy them and add full stops, commas, question marks, and exclamation marks in the correct places. Use the text provided or create your own. Tell children to scan the text to check their answers. Ask questions to check their understanding: What do fruit and vegetables give you? Play a game. Say: Shiny hair. Children then say what you can do to keep it that way: Eat meat, fish, and nuts! Children continue to play the game in small groups to consolidate their understanding of the topic. Additional task Think about activities children do and foods they eat to stay healthy. In pairs, they can ask and answer questions, or write ideas and ask their partner to read them. Remind them to focus on correct punctuation.

Think aloud to show how to identify evidence, pronounce key words and produce a complete answer. Contrast one correct and one incorrect example. CCQs: “What tells us this answer is correct?” “Which detail in the source supports it?”

Context
This is the sixth lesson of the unit ‘Keeping healthy’.
In this lesson, children will develop their reading skills by reading a short text about how to keep parts of our body healthy.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
