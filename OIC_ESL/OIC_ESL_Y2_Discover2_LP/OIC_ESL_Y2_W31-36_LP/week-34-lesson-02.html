PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Original warm-up: Ask children if they like colouring. Explain that some people feel calmer when they colour. Hand out the mindful colouring pictures and coloured pencils, and give children 5 minutes to begin colouring it in. Tell them that they probably won’t be able to finish it within this time, but whenever they are waiting or need a ‘breather’, they can take it out during the lesson. Ask them what they think about, if anything, as they colour.

Scaffold: Show a relevant picture or object; invite think–pair–share before asking for full sentences.

ICQs: “Are you thinking first or answering immediately?” “Will both partners speak?” CCQs: “What is happening in this example?” “Which example belongs to this unit? How do you know??”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher demonstration: Model the original task with a worked example: Play a flashcard game with the art and craft vocabulary from Week 34, Lesson 1. Quickly flash the cards and ask children to say the words. Then show the word cards and invite children to match the words to the pictures. Invite children to think of other things that they can make (e.g. model trains). Turn to the pictures in Oxford Discover SB 2 p.174 and play the audio in ex.A while children listen. Find out if they have ever tried a craft like origami or making mosaic pictures. Then ask a confident volunteer: Do you like making badges? Exaggerate correct question intonation. Allow them to answer: Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. Focus children’s attention on the ‘Do you like + verb with –ing’ question form. Write it on the board. 4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner application: Partners use the original lesson’s pictures, vocabulary, reading, listening, demonstration or project materials to explain, ask and respond about what do you like making?. Swap roles after two turns; require a meaningful response, not just a single word.

ICQs: “Who speaks first?” “When do you swap?” CCQs: “Does your response fit the original example?”

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: picture choices, word bank, modeled phrase, gestures and oral rehearsal. Developing: complete the target response independently with one detail. Extending: add a reason, comparison, prediction or further example.

Original support: To support less able children, allow them to ask fewer questions to fewer people. As an extension task for more able children, encourage them to find out whether people love or like making things. They could introduce a number system (e.g. 5 = love, 1 = dislike). Additional information: Week 34, Lesson 2 Example survey chart

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Original review: Children have practised using ‘like + verb with –ing’ in questions and statements. They have carried out a class survey about art and craft activities.

Exit ticket: Give one new example directly linked to what do you like making?; students respond without copying. Mark I = independent, P = prompted, N = not yet. CCQ: “What did you learn and how can you show it?”

Materials: food picture cards and two labelled sorting mats

Teacher preparation: Use the exact vocabulary, pictures and examples from the original teaching source above. Prepare the cards or prompts required for “Find Your Pair”.

I DO — teacher models: Model one complete turn using a real example from the source. Say the target language aloud: “I like ___. It is ___ because ___.” Point to the supporting picture or text.

WE DO — guided example: Complete a second example together. Ask students to identify the correct source clue, then elicit a full sentence about food and healthy choices.

YOU DO — game procedure: Give each learner a picture or word card. Learners circulate to find their match, then use both in a sentence.

ICQ: Will you show your own answer? Do you need to use the lesson pictures or words?

CCQ: Is an apple a fruit?

Success evidence: Learner uses the lesson vocabulary accurately and completes the activity with one correct example about food and healthy choices.

Support: Show two visual choices; rehearse the frame “I like ___. It is ___ because ___.” before learners respond.

Challenge: Ask for a second example or an explanation using because, while remaining on the topic of food and healthy choices.

YEAR 2 Art and craft
Week 34, Lesson 2: What do you like making?
Learning outcomes: 2.2a, 2.2b, 2.2c

Context
This is the second lesson of the unit ‘Art and craft’.
In this lesson, children will practise their speaking skills by asking and answering questions to find out what their classmates like making.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
