Measurable Learning Objectives

Lesson: 5

Measurable goals: Students demonstrate the language and skills required by the original lesson, use topic vocabulary in a meaningful response, and produce one independent piece of evidence.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Original warm-up: Begin the class with a discussion about clubs. Tell children that you are going to give them some questions to talk about in small groups. Hand out a set of questions to each group and set a time limit (e.g. 5 minutes). What clubs are you in? Which is your favourite club? Why? What clubs would you like to join? What do you think children do in an art club? Invite volunteers to share their answers with the rest of the class.

Scaffold: Show a relevant picture or object; invite think–pair–share before asking for full sentences.

ICQs: “Are you thinking first or answering immediately?” “Will both partners speak?” CCQs: “What is happening in this example?” “What material or shape was used to make this?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher demonstration: Model the original task with a worked example: If you have an art club at school, ask children what different activities they can do there. If not, ask children to imagine an art club. What different activities might it include? Write relevant vocabulary on the board. Turn to Oxford Discover SB 2 p.184 and read/listen to the story about the art club. Ask the class: Which activities does the story mention? Tell the class that they are going to set up an art club. They will need a poster to tell others about it. Elicit what sort of information might go on the poster – for instance, where the art club is, the day and time of the club, what activities you can do there, what materials are provided (or not), and the fee (if any) to join the club. Elicit useful language for giving ideas, agreeing, and disagreeing by asking: What can we say when we have an idea? What can we say if we think it’s a (not so) good idea? 4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partner application: Partners use the original lesson’s pictures, vocabulary, reading, listening, demonstration or project materials to explain, ask and respond about art club. Swap roles after two turns; require a meaningful response, not just a single word.

ICQs: “Who speaks first?” “When do you swap?” CCQs: “Does your response fit the original example?”

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent task: Complete the reading comprehension activities in Oxford Discover WB 2 pp.156–157 and the review questions on p.162. Make a list of activities for a term at an art club. Make a list of materials to bring to art club.

Students create an original response, illustration with labels, written explanation, or review-project artifact. Teacher checks accuracy against the original learning outcomes.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: picture choices, word bank, modeled phrase, gestures and oral rehearsal. Developing: complete the target response independently with one detail. Extending: add a reason, comparison, prediction or further example.

Original support: To support less able children, form mixed-ability groups. Provide some example posters to look at what information it includes together. As an extension task for more able children, ask them to include more details. For instance, what are they going to paint in the oil painting class? Are they going to paint a portrait or a landscape, or in the style of a famous artist?

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Original review: Children have made a poster about an art and craft club.

Exit ticket: Give one new example directly linked to art club; students respond without copying. Mark I = independent, P = prompted, N = not yet. CCQ: “What did you learn and how can you show it?”

Context
This is the fifth lesson of the unit ‘Art and craft’.
In this lesson, children will practise their writing skills by making a poster for an art club.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
