PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Teacher: Ask: When do we use numbers? Turn to Oxford Discover SB 2 pp.66–67. Children look at the picture and say what the girl is doing (counting). Ask children if they have a favourite number. Ask children to look around the classroom and identify any numbers they can see. Tell children that number 5 is ‘very happy’ and number 1 is ‘very sad’. Ask them to think about how they are feeling and to write the number from 1 to 5 on their whiteboard. Invite them to share their feeling score if they want to. Talk to children about how feelings change and they can change the number as the class progresses.

Students: Think silently, discuss in pairs, share one idea. ICQs: “Do you discuss first or write the final answer?” “Do both partners share?” CCQs: “Which picture or example is connected to Number fun?”

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher think-aloud: Say the operation and explain what each number means. Show a complete example, pronounce target words, and explain why it is correct. Original main activity: On the board, write the numbers: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, and 90. Elicit the numbers and ask children to repeat them with you. Invite children to count up and down in tens, twos, and threes with you. Then go around the class. Write a multiplication maths problem on the board (e.g. 5 × 2 = ?) and elicit the answer (10). Let children refer to their times tables, or any other aide memoire they have. Play a counting number game with the class. Children count, but when they get to a 3, a multiple of 3, or a number with 3 in it (e.g. 13, 23, etc.), then they say ‘Beep!’. For example: 1, 2, Beep!, 4, 5, Beep!, 7, 8, Beep!, etc. You can also add 5, multiples of 5, and numbers containing 5 to be replaced with ‘Smash!’, so you get: 1, 2, Beep!, 4, Smash!, Beep!, 7, 8, Beep!, Smash!, 11, Beep!, Beep!, etc. On the board, write some number problems for children to solve (e.g. 10 + 3 = ?, 7 – 4 = ?, 2 × 6 = ?, etc.). Children copy and solve the problems. Hand out the shape codes (or replace the shapes with emojis, if you prefer!). Challenge

CCQs: Which operation changes the amount? How do you know? “Can you point to the evidence?”

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Partners use number cards, counters and worked examples to explain, question or solve a new example related to Number fun. Partner A gives an answer; Partner B asks for evidence; switch roles and compare. ICQs: “Do both partners have a turn?” “Do you show your reason?” CCQs: Which operation changes the amount? How do you know?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent task: Solve or write a new number or word problem, then check it. Create maths problems with other symbols or pictures – perhaps characters from a game everyone is playing at the moment. Write out some problems for a partner. Keep them secret. Children tell their partner the answer and see if they can figure out the problem. As there are many ways to solve the answer, the guessing partner needs to find as many ways as possible. To make this easier, limit children to just one operation, either addition, multiplication, or subtraction.

Collect an individual oral, written or illustrated response that directly demonstrates the source learning outcomes; do not let pupils copy the teacher’s example.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Emerging: use picture choices, labeled vocabulary, gesture and an oral sentence frame. Developing: give a full answer with one accurate detail. Extending: justify, compare or design an original example. Original source differentiation: To support less able children, suggest that they only use numbers 1 to 5. As an extension task for more able children, ask them to create problems with multiples of 10. Additional information: Week 13, Lesson 5 Example: + =

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Review: Children have played and created some maths games in English.

Exit ticket: Complete one new response related to Number fun without copying. Record independent / prompted / not yet. CCQ: Which operation changes the amount? How do you know?
