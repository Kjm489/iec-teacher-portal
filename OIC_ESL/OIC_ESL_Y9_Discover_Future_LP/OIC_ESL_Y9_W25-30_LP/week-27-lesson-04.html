1. Warm-up & activate prior knowledge · 8 min

ICQ (IEC scaffold): What will you make or demonstrate today?

2. Teacher modeling — I DO · 12 min

  • Model a worked example relevant to today’s stage of the original Oxford project brief.

Teacher move (IEC scaffold): Point to the source instructions and verbalize the process.

3. Guided practice — WE DO · 15 min

  • Use the Oxford weekly brief to jointly plan the next action for session 4.

CCQ (IEC scaffold): How does today’s work connect to the original Oxford outcome?

4. Supported pair/group practice · 10 min

  • Work collaboratively on the shared Oxford project deliverable; assign specific contributions.

ICQ (IEC scaffold): Who is responsible for each part?

5. Independent practice — YOU DO · 25 min

  • Each learner independently completes or records evidence of their contribution to session 4.

Evidence (IEC scaffold): Collect one individual product, written explanation, or reflection.

6. Extension & differentiation · 10 min

  • Provide a sentence frame for learners who need support; invite confident learners to extend or justify their choices.

7. Review, assessment & exit ticket · 10 min

  • Check the progress made in session 4 and record the next action.

Exit ticket (IEC scaffold): State one thing completed today and the next step.
