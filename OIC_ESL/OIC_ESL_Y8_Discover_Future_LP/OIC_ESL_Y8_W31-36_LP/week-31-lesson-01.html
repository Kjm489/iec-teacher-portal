PERIOD 1 · 45 MINUTES — Warm-up, I DO, WE DO, Partner Practice

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Do a team vocabulary race. Give each team a set of vocabulary cards for revision. Then invite team members take turns in the ‘hot seat’. They must pick up one card and define the word for their team to guess. If the word is guessed correctly, repeat until the time is up (60 seconds). Teams can save their correctly guessed words, but must return incorrectly guessed words to you. The team with the most correctly guessed words is the winner!

Oxford introductory activity: Review any vocabulary that students were unable to define or guess.

ICQ (IEC): What is the first task and what will you produce?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Teacher model (IEC adaptation): Demonstrate one representative example from the Oxford main activity.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Elicit the main challenges facing our oceans and write them on large sheets of paper. These can be issues already discussed in the unit, or other issues that students are aware of. Display the challenges around the classroom.

Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Ask students to individually rank the challenges according to how urgent they consider each one to be. After a few minutes of quiet reflection time, invite them to get up and stand next to the challenge they consider most urgent. They should explain their reasons to the rest of the group. If necessary, divide these groups into subgroups with four to five students in each. Their assigned subgroup will be their campaign group, with whom they will work for the rest of the week’s lessons.

Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Tell students: You’re going to listen to some friends discussing a campaign idea. As soon as you know what the campaign is about, raise your hand. Play the audio in ex.2 in ODF WB 3 p.89 (track 9.01), stopping as soon as a student raises their hand. Elicit and confirm the answer – plastic pollution damaging the environment.

Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Ask students to complete ex.1 in pairs, and then discuss which picture will most likely grab someone’s attention. Play the audio again for students to complete ex.2.

Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Review the success criteria for a discussion from Week 30, Lesson 4. Then encourage pairs to evaluate the success of Ella and Jamal’s discussion. They can turn to ex.7 in ODF SB 3 p.99 to review key phrases for evaluating ideas. Play the audio one more time so they can identify any uses of these phrases.

Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Now ask students to return to their campaign group and discuss the elements of effective teamwork. If necessary, feed in some ideas. Set groups the task of setting success criteria for working as a team. They will use these criteria to evaluate their work at the end of the week.

CCQ (IEC): What detail or example supports your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Supported collaboration (IEC adaptation): Use the pair/group discussion or shared response within the Oxford task; provide a sentence frame and gradually remove it.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able students, allow them to identify key phrases in the listening transcript.

Oxford differentiation: Stretch more able students by asking them to complete ex.3 in ODF WB 3 p.89 during the listening activity.

Oxford differentiation: Encourage less confident students to focus on their strengths and identify what they can contribute to their team.

Oxford differentiation: Fast finishers can research ideas for effective teamwork for young people and share these with their team.

Oxford additional tasks: Complete exs.4–5 in ODF WB 3 p.89.

Oxford additional tasks: For inspiration, students can research some campaigns for their chosen issue.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Students have listened to a discussion about a campaign plan. They have chosen their own campaign issue to focus on, recognizing the importance of teamwork in achieving their goal.

Original OIC source sequence (reference only — expand to read)
YEAR 7 Identity
Week 1, Lesson 1: What is identity?
Learning outcomes: 7.1d, 7.2f

Context
This is the first lesson in the unit ‘Identity’.
Students will develop their listening and speaking skills by listening to how other people can be described and discussing the different ways we can identify ourselves. 
This lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, although the timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
