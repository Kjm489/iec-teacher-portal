Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Draw a two-column table on the board with the headings ‘Cause’ and ‘Result’. Give students one minute to individually write as many ideas as possible in each column. Then invite four volunteers to the board, two for each column. Ask the rest of the class to call out words for their classmates to write in the appropriate column. Review together, checking the meaning and spelling of new vocabulary as necessary.

Oxford main activity — complete original sequence: Ask pairs to complete ex.7 on p.101. Meanwhile, prepare a cause and effect matching game. Supported collaboration (IEC adaptation): Use the pair/group discussion or shared response within the Oxford task; provide a sentence frame and gradually remove it.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Give less confident students a checklist of specific features to look for in (in)formal texts.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more confident students to only use the new language from the model proposal in the matching game.

Oxford differentiation: Ask less able students to focus on just one or two new words or expressions for giving results, and to practise using these correctly in the matching game.

Oxford additional tasks: Students can create their own sentences/phrases to add to the matching activity.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Students have learned about the aim and structure of a proposal. They have identified and practised using new language for giving results.

Original OIC source sequence (reference only — expand to read)
YEAR 7 Identity
Week 1, Lesson 1: What is identity?
Learning outcomes: 7.1d, 7.2f

Context
This is the first lesson in the unit ‘Identity’.
Students will develop their listening and speaking skills by listening to how other people can be described and discussing the different ways we can identify ourselves. 
This lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, although the timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
