Oxford introductory activity: Pre-prepare sets of cards with pairs of phrases in an informal and formal register. Relate them to campaigns/proposals (e.g. ‘So come on!’ / ‘I urge you to …’). Alternatively, write them on the board in a jumbled order for the class to match together.

Oxford introductory activity: Ask students to sit in their campaign groups. Give each group a set of cards and tell them to deal the cards out between them. Explain the rules. The first student lays down a card, reading the phrase aloud and stating whether it is informal or formal language. The student who thinks they have its informal or formal counterpart then lays down their card in the same way. The rest of the group decides if this is a good pair, before continuing.

Oxford introductory activity: Give groups time to complete the activity, then go through the correct answers as a class.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less confident groups, give them a checklist of specific questions to work through when planning their tasks.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more confident students to take on a role that they are less confident about.
