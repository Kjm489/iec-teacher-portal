Supported collaboration (IEC adaptation): Use the pair/group discussion or shared response within the Oxford task; provide a sentence frame and gradually remove it.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Support less confident students by giving them a list of key websites, questions, and/or points to search for and cover in their presentation.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able students to include an evaluation of the project in their presentation.

Oxford additional tasks: Discuss which project sounds the most interesting, and what the benefits could be.

Oxford additional tasks: Watch a short video clip about ‘The Restaurant of Mistaken Orders’. Share reactions.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Students have discussed and researched ideas for intergenerational community projects. They have given a short presentation to share their learning, using repair strategies to facilitate listener understanding.
