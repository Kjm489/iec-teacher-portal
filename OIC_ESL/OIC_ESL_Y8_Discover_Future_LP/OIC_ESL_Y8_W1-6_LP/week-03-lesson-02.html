PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Students can be grouped in the way that the teacher thinks will benefit each individual the most, either through mixed-ability groups or groups made up exclusively of more able or less able students. In mixed-ability groups, less able students can be guided by more able students.

Oxford additional tasks: Complete ex.11 in ODF WB 3 p.8.

Oxford additional tasks: With permission and if all students feel comfortable, record students’ adverts and show them the footage. What do they like about their performance? What can they improve for next time? (language choice, tone of voice, confidence levels, etc.) Did all group members actively participate? What topic vocabulary did they use?

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Students have practised their speaking skills in groups and listened to the ideas of other group members. They have worked collaboratively to plan and produce an advert, using topic vocabulary and the persuasive strategies of influencers.
