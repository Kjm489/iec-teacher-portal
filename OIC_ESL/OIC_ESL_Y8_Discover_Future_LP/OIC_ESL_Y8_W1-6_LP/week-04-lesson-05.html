Section 0: Original Year 7 learning outcomes: 7.1d, 7.2f. Lesson focus: What is identity?. Success criteria: Learners demonstrate the target language or content, respond to a partner using an accurate example, and complete an independent evidence task connected to the original lesson.

Section 1: Original introductory activity: Write the word ‘identity’ on the board. Ask: How do you define your identity? Elicit students’ ideas and write them on the board (e.g. gender, age, address, nationality, physical characteristics, family and friends, interests, religion or ethnic group). Refer students to the photo and caption on p.4 of ODF SB 2. Ask: Do you agree with the caption? What are your reasons? Discuss as a class: Does the meaning of identity differ around the world? Scaffolding: Visuals, gesture, word bank and think–pair–share before whole-class responses. ICQs: “Are we thinking first?” “Does everyone share an idea?” CCQs: “What do you already know about what is identity??”

Encourage them to find additional characteristics, such as job/profession, personality, dress sense/clothes, and experiences. Highlight and discuss the meaning of any new vocabulary (e.g. courageous, enthusiastic, well dressed, survived). Ask students to do ex.2 on p.5 of ODF SB 2. Watch the Factflix video.
