Turn to ex.3 p.15 of ODF SB 3. Students are going to watch a Factflix video about how some places got their name. Ask them to write down the names of any places they hear from the first two activities (Westward Ho!, Manhattan, Wellington, Oxford, Bondi Beach, Hot Coffee).
