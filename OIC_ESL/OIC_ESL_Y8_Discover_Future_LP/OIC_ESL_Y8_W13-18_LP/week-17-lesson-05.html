PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Less confident students can write their report in pairs (from the same survey group) or in an adult-led small group.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more confident students to use comparative language and a range of connectors, such as ‘however’, ‘on the one hand’, ‘on the other hand’, and ‘furthermore’.

Oxford additional tasks: Pair up students from different survey groups. They swap reports and compare them against the survey results (if they have bar charts) to check the correct use of statistics and approximate language. Remind students to offer feedback constructively, mentioning one thing they really like about their partner’s report, and one thing that could be improved or corrected.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Students have written a formal report based on the results of their survey on their classmates’ spending habits.
