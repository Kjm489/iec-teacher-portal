PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Monitor less able students actively during the research process to ensure that they are finding the information they need from their chosen sources. Guide them by asking questions: Where do you think you can find some facts on …? What have you learned on this website? What do you think this word means?

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able students to find and add more reporting expressions to their lists and try to use them in their written work.

Oxford additional tasks: Complete exs.3–4 in ODF WB 3 p.30.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Students have looked at the organization of a descriptive essay and used a KWL chart to structure their research. They have worked together in pairs to give feedback on each other’s oral descriptions, then proceeded to write an essay plan. They have learned how to report what sources say.
