PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Independent application (IEC adaptation): Learners independently produce an answer, explanation, or product demonstrating the original OIC outcome.

Formative check (IEC): Show your own response, not a copied group answer.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Encourage less confident students to use the grammar explanation in ODF SB 3 p.127.

Oxford differentiation: To support less able students, supply them with the transcript of the conversation between Aksale and her coach.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more confident students to report other students’ comments and questions using reported speech.

Oxford differentiation: As an extension task for more able students, ask them to identify other verbs used for reporting, and to sort them according to their dependent structures.

Oxford additional tasks: Students can research Bekoji to find the answers to any of their unanswered questions.

Oxford additional tasks: Complete ex.2 in ODF SB 3 p.127 (reporting verbs and dependent structures) or exs.5–6 in ODF WB 3 p.77 (reported speech and word order).

Oxford learning review: Students have broadened their range of reporting verbs through a reading text. They have practised reporting speech in speaking and listening activities.

Original OIC source sequence (reference only — expand to read)
YEAR 7 Identity
Week 1, Lesson 1: What is identity?
Learning outcomes: 7.1d, 7.2f

Context
This is the first lesson in the unit ‘Identity’.
Students will develop their listening and speaking skills by listening to how other people can be described and discussing the different ways we can identify ourselves. 
This lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, although the timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
