PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Write these sentences on the board: “There’s an elephant in my apartment,” said Kim. “I didn’t get a good mark on my test,” said Szymon. “Games consoles are half-price online today!” said Ana. “I’m giving the class an English test tomorrow,” said Mr Tanaka. Ask children to discuss the sentences in pairs. Ask: Would you tell someone else what this person said? Why? Establish that often we want to share what someone else has said. Ask: Is it always ethical to share information? Discuss positive and negative aspects such as being proud of someone or something, sharing an important message, gossiping and being unkind, sharing a secret.

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Teach reported speech using ‘said that’, ‘told’, and ‘asked’ using your own subject knowledge and Oxford Discover SB 5 pp.75 and 83. “It’s too difficult!” said Pasha. Pasha said that it was too difficult.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: “I’m reading,” said Mrs MacIntosh. Mrs MacIntosh said that she was reading. Mikki told us that his brothers were going to the mountains. The dentist asked Saira which tooth was hurting.

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: Use the game in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.75 and the class survey in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.83. Divide the available time between each activity. Praise groups who are working well together.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: Invite children to turn the sentences in the introductory activity into reported speech. Use the game in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.75 and the class survey in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.83. Divide the available time between each activity. Praise groups who are working well together.

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, ask them to focus only on turning direct speech into reported speech until they are feel confident. Then move on to turning reported speech into direct speech. As an extension task for more able children, tell them to look at the story again in Oxford Discover SB 5 pp.72–73. Ask them to turn the direct speech into reported speech.

Oxford additional tasks: Do the activities in Oxford Discover WB 5 pp.66–67.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have learned the grammar used for reported speech. They have practised ‘said that’, ‘told’, and ‘asked’.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.
