PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Play a game. Divide the class into two teams. Ask five volunteers from each team to come to the front of the class. Give them an animal name. Tell them to keep their animal secret. List for Team 1: cat, elephant, horse, snake, whale. List for Team 2: lion, mouse, dog, bee, dolphin. Set a timer. Ask Team 1 children to imitate their animal, one at a time. The watching team guess which animal it is. When all five have been guessed, record Team 1’s time. Repeat with Team 2. The team which guesses all the animals the fastest is the winner. Ask: Which of the animals in the game has four legs, has large tusks, and lives in families? The answer is the animal in today’s lesson (an elephant).

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Do the listening activity in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.84. Discuss as a class: What have you learned about elephant communication? Teach pronoun agreement, using your subject knowledge and Oxford Discover SB 5 p.85.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: A singular noun matches a singular pronoun and verb. The boy is reading a book. He loves it. Everybody/nobody/anybody/somebody likes cheese. Does anybody want to come to the cinema with us?

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: Tell children to write a sentence with a subject and a verb on their whiteboard. Ask them to get up and swap their board with someone across the room. Their partner should write a related sentence using a pronoun and a different verb. Example: Child 1: My dog barks every night. Child 2: He doesn’t like the dark. Check and correct sentences. Repeat.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: A plural noun matches a plural pronoun and verb. The girls are playing basketball. They play every Saturday. Here are the boys. Do they want to watch TV? Tell children to write a sentence with a subject and a verb on their whiteboard. Ask them to get up and swap their board with someone across the room. Their partner should write a related sentence using a pronoun and a different verb. Example: Child 1: My dog barks every night. Child 2: He doesn’t like the dark. Check and correct sentences. Repeat.

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, review relevant vocabulary about elephants before doing the listening exercise. Put keywords on the board. As an extension task for more able children, ask them to see how far they can communicate with each other, using their voice to make different types of animal sounds. Then research the distances animal noises can travel in real life.

Oxford additional tasks: Complete the activities in Oxford Discover WB 5 p.77. Look at a book you are reading in English. Find examples of pronoun agreement.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have listened for details in an exercise about elephant communication. They have studied pronoun agreement.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.
