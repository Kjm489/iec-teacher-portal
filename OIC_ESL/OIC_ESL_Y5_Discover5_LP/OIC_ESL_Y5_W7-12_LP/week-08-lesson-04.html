PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Ask children to write ‘EXCITING’ and ‘BORING’ on their whiteboards. Ask them to write as many activities as they can under each word. Share opinions as a class. Ask: What do you think? Do you agree with [child’s name]? Why? Invite children to think of different ways people share their opinions. Write them on the board (family or friends, newspapers, TV shows, reviews, websites or blogs, social media). Show real-world examples of opinions in print or on screen to the class. Ask: Is it important to think about how you share your opinion? Why? Discuss the balance between kindness and honesty. Ask: What do you do if you don’t agree with someone’s opinion? (e.g. be respectful of others, be calm and polite, think about it from their point of view) Explain that one way of sharing opinions is by writing an opinion essay.

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Use Oxford Discover SB 5 pp.64–65 exs.A–C to teach how to write an opinion essay.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: Tell children to plan their own opinion essay about transport in the future. Use Oxford Discover WB 5 p.59, or create a mind map or a different kind of graphic organizer for the main idea and supporting details. Remind children to give supporting reasons for their opinions. This plan will be used in the next lesson.

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: Use Oxford Discover SB 5 pp.64–65 exs.A–C to teach how to write an opinion essay. Tell children to plan their own opinion essay about transport in the future. Use Oxford Discover WB 5 p.59, or create a mind map or a different kind of graphic organizer for the main idea and supporting details. Remind children to give supporting reasons for their opinions. This plan will be used in the next lesson.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: Tell children to plan their own opinion essay about transport in the future. Use Oxford Discover WB 5 p.59, or create a mind map or a different kind of graphic organizer for the main idea and supporting details. Remind children to give supporting reasons for their opinions. This plan will be used in the next lesson.

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, work in a small group to plan the essay with an adult. Encourage less confident children to voice their opinion by reminding them that there is no ‘wrong’ opinion and that everyone is entitled to share their point of view. Challenge more able children by giving them a topic to write about which is linked to a transport development in your country (such as an airport expansion or a new motorway). Additional information: Week 8, Lesson 4 Transport in the future [Opening statement and opinion] In my opinion, bicycles are the best transport for the future! I think that by 2030, bicycles will become more popular across the world. [Second paragraph] Cities will build more bicycle lanes to make cycling safer and quicker. They will learn from cities that already make cycling safe and easy, like Copenhagen, Amsterdam, and Montreal. This is a good thing because we won't need as many cars, so there will be less traffic. [Conclusion] Finally, I think that bicycles are an important way for us to protect the planet. Bicycles don't use any energy except for human legs, so they are very green. We won't need so many cars on the roads. I believe that, in the future, people will be healthier and the planet will be greener because of bicycles!

Oxford additional tasks: Complete the activities in Oxford Discover WB 5 p.58. Research some themes related to transport in the future. This can be done before planning the opinion essay. Topics could include self-driving cars or the future of travel in cities.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have discussed opinions in the world today, analysed an opinion essay, and planned their own opinion essay.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.

Transport in the future

[Opening statement and opinion]

In my opinion, bicycles are the best transport for the future! I think that by 2030, bicycles will become more popular across the world.

[Second paragraph]

Cities will build more bicycle lanes to make cycling safer and quicker. They will learn from cities that already make cycling safe and easy, like Copenhagen, Amsterdam, and Montreal. This is a good thing because we won't need as many cars, so there will be less traffic.

[Conclusion]

Finally, I think that bicycles are an important way for us to protect the planet. Bicycles don't use any energy except for human legs, so they are very green. We won't need so many cars on the roads. I believe that, in the future, people will be healthier and the planet will be greener because of bicycles!
