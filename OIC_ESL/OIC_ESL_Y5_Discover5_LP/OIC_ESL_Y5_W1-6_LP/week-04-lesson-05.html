PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Set a clearly visible timer on the board, counting up from 0:00. Say the following list of instructions and check for understanding before saying ‘Start’. When I finish these instructions, I will say ‘Start’. Close your eyes. Pretend to brush your teeth. Brush the way you do at home. When you have finished, open your eyes and stand up. Write down the time that you finish on your whiteboard. Start. When everyone has stood up, ask children to hold up their boards. Ask: How long do you think you should brush your teeth? The standard is two minutes, twice a day. Ask: How can we measure two minutes? (e.g. use a clock, play a song). Explain that children will be thinking about how to follow instructions today.

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Play the listening exercise in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.34. Alternatively, search online for ‘audio instructions brush teeth’ to find a suitable audio or video clip giving instructions on how to brush teeth properly. Ask: What is the purpose of this audio? Who do you think the audience is? Why? Give each child, or pairs of children, a slip of paper with one of the steps from the instructions.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: Tell children to find the other steps and put themselves in the correct order. When they have finished, ask each ‘step pair’ to read their instructions, in order to check. Ask: What have you learned about brushing your teeth?

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: When they have finished, ask each ‘step pair’ to read their instructions, in order to check.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: Provide a range of materials with different types of instructions: numbered, using sequencing words, and bullet point lists. Do not discuss these differences at this point. Examples to share and discuss: Oxford Discover SB 5 p.25 ex.D (numbering), p.25 ex.C (bullet points). Also search online for ‘KS2 model instruction texts’, and use non-fiction books, simple recipes, and craft instructions. In pairs, children look at different examples and discuss the features of instructions. Discuss as a class. What kind of verb is being used? (imperative) What different ways can you show the order of the steps?

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: In the main activity, pair up more confident with less confident children when analysing the different examples of instructions. For the additional task, give more able children a specific audience for their instructions (such as very young children, or people newly arrived from another country).

Oxford additional tasks: Additional tasks, focusing on purpose and audience: Create a poster or a video with instructions on how to brush teeth correctly. Create a list of instructions on a topic of choice.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have learned how to follow instructions using teeth brushing as an example. They have identified different ways to structure instructions.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.

Context
This is the fifth lesson of the unit ‘What do you know about teeth?’.
In this lesson, children will develop their listening and reading skills by improving their understanding of how to write and follow instructions. They will have the opportunity to link this topic to their daily life. 
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
