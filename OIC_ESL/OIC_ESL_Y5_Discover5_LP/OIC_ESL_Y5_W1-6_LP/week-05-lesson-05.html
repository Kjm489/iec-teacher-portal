PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Ask children to slowly stretch their arms to the ceiling, out to the side, and then touch their toes before sitting down. Explain to children that this is the last lesson in this unit before the review week. Today, they will write a short report, with three sections, about one aspect of human or animal teeth. Break down the steps needed by using Oxford Discover SB 5 pp.44–45 and/or Oxford Discover WB 5 pp.38–39, and by reviewing this process learned from Week 2, Lesson 5. Ask children to think about their time management in Week 2, Lesson 5. Did they manage it well? Write the stages on the board. brainstorm (mind map) outline draft check finalize

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Provide a small range of sources for children to use, including the video in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.45. This should include any texts and photos used during the unit, as well as the children’s own work. Do not provide any other new materials, as children will have very little time for further research. Use the template in Oxford Discover WB 5 p.39.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: Ask children to think about how much time they have for each stage and monitor it themselves. Remind them that the lesson is not long! Encourage children to take a few breaths before they move on to each new stage.

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: At the end of the lesson, ask children to slowly stretch their arms to the ceiling, out to the side, and then touch their toes. Remind children that this task was challenging, and praise their effort and attitude.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: After the drafting stage, ask children to check each other’s work and provide feedback (one thing to praise and one thing to improve) before finalizing. Check spelling and punctuation. At the end of the lesson, ask children to slowly stretch their arms to the ceiling, out to the side, and then touch their toes. Remind children that this task was challenging, and praise their effort and attitude.

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, give them a choice of 2–3 topics with suggested ideas, and a range of sentence starters, so that they can focus on drafting their report. Less confident children can write a report with two sections instead of three. Challenge more confident children to use modals of ability, modals of certainty, and the suffixes ‘–ment’ and ‘–ing’ in their writing. Give more able children a mentor role in helping others when they have finished.

Oxford additional tasks: Provide more time to expand the report, adding illustrations and data such as diagrams or graphs. Children can also review the report and edit it to include more examples of the vocabulary and grammar learned in this unit.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have learned how to structure the writing process, and how to manage the time available to complete a report about teeth.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.
