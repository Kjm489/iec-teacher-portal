PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Play the Big Question 1 video on Oxford Discover p.7. Ask children to look out for two different types of days out (museum, animal park). Ask children to think of one good ‘day out’ activity and discuss their ideas in pairs. Share ideas as a whole class.

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Use the reading text in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.106. Remind children that they read this text in the ‘Experiencing other cultures’ unit. Ask them to reread it now.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: Ask children to tell a partner four things you can do for a day out in Brazil. Check answers as a class – visit the rainforest, visit famous cities, explore culture, and discover traditions and customs. Draw a mind map or table on the board and invite children to add details from the text to each of these four categories. Encourage the use of the unreal conditional to discuss what type of day out children would like to have in Brazil. Invite them to share their ideas in pairs.

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: Ask children to tell a partner four things you can do for a day out in Brazil. Check answers as a class – visit the rainforest, visit famous cities, explore culture, and discover traditions and customs.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: Discuss: What days out can we enjoy in our local area? Write a list on the board. Divide children into small groups to share ideas and opinions about days out they have had in the past. Ask them to think about whether these cost money or were free, and why they enjoyed them. To close the lesson, ask: Do you think you have to spend money for ‘a good day out’? Discuss as a class.

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children before the group discussion about past days out, create a list of examples on the board. Review related vocabulary linked to opinions and money. Challenge more able and confident children to revisit the present perfect for questions and short answers in their group discussions: e.g. Have you ever …? Yes, I have. / No, I haven’t.

Oxford additional tasks: Look through all the units covered so far in Oxford Discover SB 5. Tell pairs of children to use the photos and illustrations to list as many different types of days out as they can.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have read about different kinds of days out and chosen their preferences. They have discussed days out they have had in the past.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.
