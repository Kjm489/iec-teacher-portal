PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Challenge children, in pairs, to complete three word tasks in five minutes. Each pair needs a dictionary, a whiteboard, and a marker pen. Set a timer. Instruct pairs to look up the meaning of ‘economical’ and write it on their whiteboard. While children do this, write on the board: ‘cheap’, ‘economical’, ‘expensive’, and ‘budget-friendly’. Ask pairs to identify and write the ‘odd one out’ on their whiteboard. Ask pairs to write a sentence on their whiteboard using one of these words. Stop the timer when the first pair raises their hand. Give other pairs time to finish. Then check answers. (The odd one out is ‘expensive’.)

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Tell children that they are going to write their own advert for a day trip. This will be a trip for an end-of-term treat, open to all children in the school. The trip can be a day out at any location, or any activity they wish. Use ‘Two boat trips’ and the leaflets/brochures from Week 26, Lesson 4 as model texts.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: Identify key information which needs to be included in an advert (e.g. activity, price, location, time). Write success criteria on the board. Challenge children to find useful phrases in the leaflets/brochures which make the day out sound attractive. Elicit examples and write them on the board.

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: Give children time to read their advert out loud to a partner, identifying and correcting mistakes.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: Task children to plan and write their own short advert. Give children time to read their advert out loud to a partner, identifying and correcting mistakes. Invite children to share their advert with the class. Question them using concepts and vocabulary learned during this unit: e.g. Is your day out good value for money? Why? Does this activity accept electronic or cash payments? Can I pay in euros, pounds, or dollars?

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able children to choose and research an activity or attraction which they are not familiar with to extend their vocabulary and experience.

Oxford additional tasks: Encourage children to check and polish their text. When they are happy with the final draft, they can create a poster or leaflet version of their advert.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have written an advert for an end-of-term day out.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.
