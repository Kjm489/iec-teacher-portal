PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Play the class a video clip of children raising money for a good cause. Ask: What do you think these children are doing? Why? Say that this lesson will give them the opportunity to think about donating money and time to different good causes. Explain the words ‘cause’ and ‘charity’ and the difference between the two, as well as the relationship between ‘raising’ and ‘donating’ money.

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Ask children if they have ever donated their money or time to support a charity. Which local charities have you supported? Have you supported any charities which work in other countries? Which of your talents and skills did you use?

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: Celebrate children’s contribution to their local community or the global community. Form groups of three or four children. Tell each group that they have a sum of money and a number of hours to donate to local causes (suggest: $100 or equivalent and two days). Help children to understand that donating time is as valuable as donating money. Use the speaking activity in Oxford Discover SB 5 p.146, or select four specific local causes or charities. Briefly introduce them and check understanding of their roles. Children must discuss and decide as a group which cause to support – they can only choose one. Give each group access to the internet and the website address for each cause to help them make their decision.

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: Form groups of three or four children. Tell each group that they have a sum of money and a number of hours to donate to local causes (suggest: $100 or equivalent and two days). Help children to understand that donating time is as valuable as donating money. Children must discuss and decide as a group which cause to support – they can only choose one. Give each group access to the internet and the website address for each cause to help them make their decision. Remind children that this is a group decision. They should give and receive their opinions, and seek to influence their group members, with respect and tact. All team members should have the opportunity to contribute. Invite each group to present the results of their discussion to the class. Hold a class vote.

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: Remind children that this is a group decision. They should give and receive their opinions, and seek to influence their group members, with respect and tact. All team members should have the opportunity to contribute. Challenge children to practise using the unreal conditional as they talk: e.g. If I had this money, I would donate it to … because … / If I had two days free this weekend, I would help … By thinking again about this conditional tense, they are helping to build their long-term memory of this learning. Invite each group to present the results of their discussion to the class. Hold a class vote. Discuss as a class: Was it difficult to make a decision? Why / Why not? What factors did you consider? What are the advantages and disadvantages of giving money or time to only one cause, rather than splitting it?

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, create mixed-ability groups. Ask more able children to work with them to construct their points, using the unreal conditional where possible. Challenge more confident children by nominating them to present the results of their group discussion.

Oxford additional tasks: In pairs, ask children to write a short list of ways to raise money to donate to a good cause. Make a poster to illustrate this.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have used the unreal conditional to discuss donating money and time to good causes. They have held a class vote.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.

YEAR 5 Using money responsibly
Week 26, Lesson 2: Donating money and time
Learning outcomes: 5.2b, 5.2f

Context
This is the seventh lesson of the unit ‘Using money responsibly’.
In this lesson, children will develop their speaking skills by putting their learning of the unreal conditional into further practice. They will have the opportunity to explore how donating money and time has a positive impact on their community.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
