PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory activity: Tell the class that exploring is often a group activity. Groups of explorers live in small spaces together, such as a boat, a spaceship, or a tent. Team members must support each other for the journey to be successful. We can learn from this in our own activities. Remind children that it is good to share positive thoughts about classmates. Invite them to share the name of another child who: has worked hard this week has said something interesting in class has helped them (or someone else) with their work has contributed positively in another way (explain why). Share your own positive thoughts about the class. Celebrate each other’s strengths. Ask children to notice their feelings following this exercise (e.g. proud, positive, happy).

ICQ: What will you do first? Are you listening, speaking, reading, or writing?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford teacher demonstration: Choose between the following two activities: Use ‘Planet SinRota’ in Oxford Discover SB 5 pp.58–59 (from Week 8, Lesson 2).

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task and think aloud before learners attempt it.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford guided practice: Invite children to tell you what they remember about the story. Divide children into groups of three or four. Give each group a large Venn diagram. One circle should be labelled ‘Planet SinRota’ and the overlapping circle labelled ‘Earth’.

CCQ: Which words or evidence from the Oxford task support your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford collaborative practice: Divide children into groups of three or four. Give each group a large Venn diagram. One circle should be labelled ‘Planet SinRota’ and the overlapping circle labelled ‘Earth’. Invite each group to report back to the class. Ask: Where would you rather live: Planet SinRota or Earth? Why?

Scaffolding: Provide a short model or sentence frame, then gradually withdraw support.

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford independent practice: Tell children they are explorers finding out about the people, objects, and characteristics of each planet. They must put their findings into the diagram. Invite each group to report back to the class. Ask: Where would you rather live: Planet SinRota or Earth? Why? Choose a non-fiction text about space exploration to read and discuss in pairs or small groups. Suggestion: choose a section of the Oxford Reading Tree book Maggie Aderin-Pocock: Space Scientist (Author: Jo Nelson; Illustrator: Charley Fears).

Independent evidence: Each learner demonstrates the original learning outcome independently.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, create mixed-ability groups. Encourage more able children to support their peers to complete the diagram.

Oxford additional tasks: Watch a short video about astronauts working in the International Space Station. Find out when it will be passing over your location. Search for ‘NASA Spot the station’. Find out how to become an astronaut. What skills do astronauts need? How do you apply?

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have read a text about exploring space. They have used a Venn diagram to categorize information about two planets.

Assessment: Check the stated OIC learning outcome using individual spoken, written, or practical evidence.
