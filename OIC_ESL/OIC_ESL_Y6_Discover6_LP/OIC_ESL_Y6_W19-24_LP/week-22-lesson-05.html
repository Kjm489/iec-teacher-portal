PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford task — independent application: Explain how to make the bird feeder. Encourage children to adapt the plan if possible, to take into account their own ideas.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, take a step-by-step approach to making the bird feeder. Provide simple one-line instructions and feedback at different stages throughout the task.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able children to adapt their bird feeder to include an additional element, such as a bird bath. Provide suitable materials for them to experiment with.

Oxford additional tasks: In pairs, talk about future life plans using expressions of probability.

Oxford additional tasks: Design and write clear instructions for a bird feeder which uses recycled materials.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have planned and made a bird feeder. They have talked about the activity using expressions of probability.

Assessment: Check an individual response against the original learning outcome; note achieved, developing, or needs reteaching.
