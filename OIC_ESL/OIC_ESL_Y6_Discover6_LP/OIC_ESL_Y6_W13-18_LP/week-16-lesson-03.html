PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 1 · 45 MINUTES — Warm-up, I DO, WE DO, Pair Practice

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory task: Do the code activity in Oxford Discover SB 6 p.93 or use the code provided. Give children a clue if they find it difficult.

Oxford introductory task: Ask children to discuss: Is a code a type of language? (No, it isn’t a true language. A code is used to express an existing language in a different form.)

ICQ: What is the first action you will take?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford task — teacher models: Do a reading comprehension activity. Use ‘Talking about Language’ in Oxford Discover SB 6 pp.92–94 or ‘Stop-Motion Animation’ in Bright Ideas CB 6 p.25.

Oxford task — teacher models: Instruct children to skim the title, headings (if used), highlighted words, and illustrations or photos. Ask: How do you think this text reflects the lesson topic ‘Communication with or without words’? What types of communication might be discussed in this text?

Modeling move: Demonstrate one worked example, verbalizing the reasoning before learners try.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford task — guided practice: Give children time to read the text independently or in pairs. Provide dictionaries for support. Remind children to identify the main idea of each section as well as any supporting details.

Oxford task — guided practice: Direct children to complete the comprehension activities in Oxford Discover SB 6 p.94 or Bright Ideas CB 6 p.25. Observe and support children as they work.

CCQ: Which example or detail from the task supports your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Collaborative practice (IEC scaffold): In pairs, rehearse the task demonstrated in guided practice; compare responses and give one reason for each choice. This is an IEC teaching adaptation, not a separate Oxford activity.

ICQ: Will both partners contribute and listen?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford task — independent application: During the time that children take to complete the reading and comprehension activities, encourage them to take mindful moments to take a break and reflect on their work so far.

Oxford task — independent application: Check answers as a class. Discuss any new or difficult vocabulary.

Oxford task — independent application: Ask: What types of communication were discussed in the text? Did you learn anything new about ways to express yourself, with or without words?

Independent evidence: Learners complete an individual response demonstrating the original OIC learning outcomes.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, work in a small group with adult support. Work through each section of the text, explaining new vocabulary and discussing any difficult concepts. After reading, see if children can independently complete the comprehension tasks. Have adult support available for this if required.

Oxford differentiation: As an extension task for more able children, ask them to reflect on the common English phrase: Actions are more powerful than words. Ask: Do we communicate more through what we do than through what we say?

Context
This is the third lesson of the unit ‘Using language effectively’.
In this lesson, children will develop their reading skills by building on their prior learning to understand a non-fiction text. They will also have the opportunity to practise using codes.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
