PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 1 · 45 MINUTES — Warm-up, I DO, WE DO, Pair Practice

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory task: Display photos of bridges and/or tunnels of the future. Search online for ‘futuristic bridge (or tunnel) photo’ to find unusual and futuristic designs.

Oxford introductory task: Ask: Do you like these ideas? Do you think the future is exciting? Why / Why not?

Oxford introductory task: Tell children that today they will learn new grammar to help them talk about the future.

ICQ: What is the first action you will take?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford task — teacher models: Take the first 15 minutes to teach either the future continuous or the present continuous, as appropriate to the class.

Oxford task — teacher models: future continuous (Oxford Discover SB 6 p.13) Next Sunday, I will be visiting my grandparents. Is Anna going to be taking her exam tomorrow?

Oxford task — teacher models: present continuous used for future plans (Bright Ideas CB 6 p.11) I’m meeting Rishad at the park this afternoon. They’re having a party next week.

Oxford task — teacher models: Divide children into pairs. Give each pair a flashcard game containing two sets of cards. One set should have different times set in the future, such as, ‘this evening’, ‘next Thursday’, or ‘in two months’ time’. The other set should have activities such as ‘play basketball’, ‘climb a volcano’, or ‘create a rock collection’. Try to include at least ten different times and fifteen different activities.

Modeling move: Demonstrate one worked example, verbalizing the reasoning before learners try.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford task — guided practice: In pairs, children select one card from the TIME pile and one from the ACTIVITY pile. They use them to create an affirmative or a negative statement: e.g. Next week, I will be cooking dinner for my parents. / I’m not cooking dinner for my parents next week. Children should repeat this exercise many times.

Oxford task — guided practice: As they practise, remind children that when we practise something regularly, the neurons involved in that activity send messages back and forth, and wire together. This helps solidify their learning into long-term memory.

Oxford task — guided practice: Tell children they can now talk about their own future activities and goals.

Oxford task — guided practice: Ask: What’s your dream future? Invite volunteers to share their future goals.

CCQ: Which example or detail from the task supports your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Collaborative practice (IEC scaffold): In pairs, rehearse the task demonstrated in guided practice; compare responses and give one reason for each choice. This is an IEC teaching adaptation, not a separate Oxford activity.

ICQ: Will both partners contribute and listen?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford task — independent application: Children write one or more sentences, beginning ‘In my dream future, I …’.

Oxford task — independent application: In my dream future, I’ll be working on the space station.

Oxford task — independent application: In my dream future, I’m acting on a big stage!

Oxford task — independent application: Optionally, display children’s dreams in a creative way. They could write their sentences inside stars, as leaves on a tree, or other shapes representing growth and achievement.

Independent evidence: Learners complete an individual response demonstrating the original OIC learning outcomes.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, pair them with a more able learner in the flashcard game.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more confident children by asking them to create questions as well as statements in the flashcard game.

Oxford differentiation: As an extension task for more able children, they can write a paragraph to answer the question: What will children’s lives be like in the year 2075?

Oxford additional tasks: Complete the exercises in Oxford Discover WB 6 pp.6–7 or Bright Ideas AB 6 p.8.

Oxford additional tasks: Think about (or research) what is happening in local and international news. Create sentences or a short news report using the appropriate future tense.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have learned how to talk about future activities using either the future or present continous tense. They have written about their own personal ambitions.

Assessment: Check an individual response against the original learning outcome; note achieved, developing, or needs reteaching.
