Collaborative practice (IEC scaffold): In pairs, rehearse the task demonstrated in guided practice; compare responses and give one reason for each choice. This is an IEC teaching adaptation, not a separate Oxford activity.

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford task — independent application: Discuss different strategies for linking information in note form (mind maps) and in formal writing (connectors).

Oxford task — independent application: Write their sentences on the board and highlight the connectors.

Oxford task — independent application: Invite them to explain their thinking behind the connector that they used.

Independent evidence: Learners complete an individual response demonstrating the original OIC learning outcomes.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, provide simple pairs of sentences. Ask them to choose the most appropriate connector for each pair.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able children by asking them to think about where each connector could be placed in a sentence (at the start, in the middle, at the end).

Oxford additional tasks: Complete the activities in Oxford Discover WB 6 p.17 or Bright Ideas AB 6 p.45.

Oxford additional tasks: Look at non-fiction books about bridges and tunnels. Work with a partner to identify connectors. Think about why the author has used them. Make a list of connectors to use in future writing tasks.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have learned how to use connectors to link supporting ideas related to the unit topic.

Assessment: Check an individual response against the original learning outcome; note achieved, developing, or needs reteaching.
