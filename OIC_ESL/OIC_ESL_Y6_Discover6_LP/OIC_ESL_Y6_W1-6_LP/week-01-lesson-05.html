PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford task — independent application: Role-play exchanges using rude or polite responses. Ask volunteers to give you an opinion: e.g. Apples are tastier than oranges. Respond to each child either rudely or politely.

Oxford task — independent application: Invite the class to think about how the volunteers felt when they were spoken to rudely or politely. Then ask the volunteers to share with the class how they did feel.

Oxford task — independent application: Discuss how different ways of speaking to each other can affect teamworking in class by making children feel valued or disrespected.

Oxford task — independent application: Divide children into pairs. They practise using these expressions when discussing facts and opinions about bridges and tunnels.

Independent evidence: Learners complete an individual response demonstrating the original OIC learning outcomes.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Encourage less able and less confident children by allowing them to talk about any subject when practising the expressions, not just bridges and tunnels.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more confident children by asking them to think about body language and facial expressions.

Oxford differentiation: As an extension task for more able children, ask them to research some polite customs in different countries and talk about how they differ from the customs in your country.

Oxford additional tasks: Write a playscript, including rude and polite responses, for a campaign to promote politeness in school. This can be performed to other classes.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have learned new expressions to help them resolve differences of opinion or to correct their peers’ mistakes in a polite way.

Assessment: Check an individual response against the original learning outcome; note achieved, developing, or needs reteaching.

YEAR 6 Bridges and tunnels 
Week 1, Lesson 5: Bridges and tunnels in our daily lives 
Learning outcomes: 6.1e, 6.2f, 6.2g

Context
This is the fifth lesson of the unit ‘Bridges and tunnels’.
In this lesson, children will develop their listening and speaking skills by listening for key words and by learning how to politely correct someone. 
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
