PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 1 · 45 MINUTES — Warm-up, I DO, WE DO, Pair Practice

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory task: Show children videos or photos of interesting bridges and tunnels to create excitement in the topic. Use the Big Question video from Oxford Discover SB 6 p.7 or search online for ‘bridges tunnels photo pool’.

Oxford introductory task: Show a short video of the construction of a bridge or tunnel. Search online for ‘timelapse video bridge (or tunnel) construction’.

Oxford introductory task: Teach suitable topic-related vocabulary for your class. Ideas: ‘connect’, ‘isolated’, ‘journey’, ‘obstacle’, ‘passenger’, ‘reach’, ‘traffic’, ‘underground’.

ICQ: What is the first action you will take?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford task — teacher models: Invite children to discuss answers to the following questions in groups of two or three. Children note their answers on their whiteboards. Ask them to think about different places, types of transport, and reasons for travelling. Remind them to practise new words.

Oxford task — teacher models: Ask:

Oxford task — teacher models: Why do we build bridges and tunnels?

Modeling move: Demonstrate one worked example, verbalizing the reasoning before learners try.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford task — guided practice: Are there any bridges and tunnels near us?

Oxford task — guided practice: What famous bridges or tunnels can you name in our country or in other countries?

Oxford task — guided practice: Where have you travelled to using a bridge or a tunnel?

Oxford task — guided practice: Invite three children from different groups to sit at the front of the class.

CCQ: Which example or detail from the task supports your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Collaborative practice (IEC scaffold): In pairs, rehearse the task demonstrated in guided practice; compare responses and give one reason for each choice. This is an IEC teaching adaptation, not a separate Oxford activity.

ICQ: Will both partners contribute and listen?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford task — independent application: Pretend you are a television host. You are interviewing a group of travel experts on TV. Ask the same questions to the three children at the front of the class.

Oxford task — independent application: Tell the class they must make notes about the interviews on their whiteboard. You will be looking for children who show the best attitude to listening.

Oxford task — independent application: After interviewing the ‘experts’, take feedback from the class about what they understood.

Oxford task — independent application: Praise children who showed a positive attitude to listening in this activity.

Independent evidence: Learners complete an individual response demonstrating the original OIC learning outcomes.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children, provide photo prompts for the discussion activity (with captions if needed).

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able children by giving them a critical-thinking question. For example: Do you think the impact of bridges and tunnels on our city is positive or negative? Why?

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more confident children by asking them a question in the class interview which they have not prepared for.

Oxford additional tasks: Complete the Big Question in Oxford Discover WB 6 p.2.

Oxford additional tasks: Teachers using Bright Ideas may wish to link to the topic of day trips, using Bright Ideas CB 6 p.16 and Bright Ideas AB 6 p.13. Invite children to think about how we use bridges and tunnels when we go on a day trip.

Oxford additional tasks: Record a short interview in pairs or groups, using the main class activity as a model.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have shared their knowledge of bridges and tunnels in small groups and as part of classroom interviews. They have practised using new topic-related words.

Assessment: Check an individual response against the original learning outcome; note achieved, developing, or needs reteaching.
