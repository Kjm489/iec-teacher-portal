Original lesson-stage directions (reference)

Stage 1: 1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min Original introductory activity: Provide children with plastic or wooden building blocks and ask them to make a bridge or a tunnel. It can be any size or shape. Teachers using Bright Ideas may prefer to ask children to create a game using sports equipment such as balls, hoops, and bean bags. Ask children to keep their creations safe. Explain that today’s lesson is about providing and following instructions. Scaffolding: Visuals, gesture, word bank and think–pair–share before whole-class responses. ICQs: “Are we thinking first?” “Does everyone share an idea?” CCQs: “What do you already know about following instructions?”

Stage 2: 2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min Teacher model grounded in the original activity: Do the listening activity in Oxford Discover SB 6 p.22 or Bright Ideas CB 6 p.15. Teach expressions for instructions, such as: The first step is … Next … Then … After that … Finally … What do you do after that? Then what? Tell children to write bullet points about how they made their bridge or tunnel (or how to play their game) on a whiteboard. Encourage half the children to walk around the classroom and to ask the rest of the children (seated) about their creations. Children should ask: How did you build this? / How do you play this game? The seated children should respond by providing instructions. The first speaker can then ask more questions to check understanding. After five to ten minutes, switch roles. Observe children as they walk and encourage less confident children to join in. Hold a class vote or use your own observations to choose: the best creation/game the most clearly explained instructions. Demonstrate one complete example aloud, highlight useful language, show a visual or text example, and think aloud about how to check accuracy. CCQs: “What did I do first?” “Which detail proves my answer?”
