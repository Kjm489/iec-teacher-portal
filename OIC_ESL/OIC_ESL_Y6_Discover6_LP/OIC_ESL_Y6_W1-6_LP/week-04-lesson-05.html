PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 1 · 45 MINUTES — Warm-up, I DO, WE DO, Pair Practice

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford introductory task: Show a video or photos of exciting and beautiful geological formations. Search online for ‘strange geological formations’ or ‘amazing geological phenomena’.

Oxford introductory task: Talk about any geological formations children have seen themselves (such as caves, cliffs, fossils, mountains, or valleys).

ICQ: What is the first action you will take?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford task — teacher models: Divide children into pairs.

Oxford task — teacher models: Tell children they will plan and conduct interviews with a geologist. They will practise both roles. They will have ten minutes to plan questions and five minutes to conduct the first interview.

Oxford task — teacher models: Ask each pair to choose a new discovery from this list:

Oxford task — teacher models: a new element, a large cave, an active volcano, a new energy source, a fossil, or a new type of rock on Mars

Modeling move: Demonstrate one worked example, verbalizing the reasoning before learners try.

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford task — guided practice: Tell children to create a list of questions on their whiteboard to ask a geologist about this discovery. Remind them to include modal verbs.

Oxford task — guided practice: Children may use Oxford Discover SB 6 p.34 ex.C for reference.

Oxford task — guided practice: Challenge them to use as much vocabulary and grammar from this week’s lessons as they can. Remind them that this helps to strengthen their neural connections.

Oxford task — guided practice: Practise the interview in pairs for five minutes.

CCQ: Which example or detail from the task supports your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Collaborative practice (IEC scaffold): In pairs, rehearse the task demonstrated in guided practice; compare responses and give one reason for each choice. This is an IEC teaching adaptation, not a separate Oxford activity.

ICQ: Will both partners contribute and listen?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES
PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford task — independent application: At the end of the interview, play a short piece of happy music for 30 seconds and ask children to move to the music.

Oxford task — independent application: Conduct a second interview for five minutes. This time, children should switch roles.

Oxford task — independent application: Repeat the music break.

Oxford task — independent application: As a class, evaluate how this activity went. Ask: Is it easier to be the person asking or answering the questions? Why? What new words or phrases did you need?

Oxford task — independent application: Invite children to share useful phrases they used. Write them on the board.

Independent evidence: Learners complete an individual response demonstrating the original OIC learning outcomes.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less confident children, pair them with a more confident child. The less able child should take the role of interviewer first, so that the more confident child can model answering the questions.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able children by asking them to choose a topic which is not on the list above. This may be related to a real-life situation, such as a recent volcanic eruption, earthquake, or fossil discovery. Provide news and magazine articles to help them prepare.

Oxford additional tasks: In pairs, record an interview on video. Pretend to be on a news programme. Think about the audience (young children, children of the same age, or adults).

Oxford additional tasks: Invite a geologist to speak to the class about their job, or research different types of jobs for geologists online.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have planned and conducted an interview with a geologist, practising the roles of interviewer and interviewee. They have practised all the vocabulary and grammar learned this week.

Assessment: Check an individual response against the original learning outcome; note achieved, developing, or needs reteaching.

Context
This is the fifth lesson of the unit ‘Inside the Earth’.
In this lesson, children will develop their speaking skills by applying the vocabulary and grammar they have learned in Lessons 1–4 to an interview situation. 
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
