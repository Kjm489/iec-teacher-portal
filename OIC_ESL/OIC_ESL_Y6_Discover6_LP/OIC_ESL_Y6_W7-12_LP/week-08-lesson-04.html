Additional information: Week 8, Lesson 4

Insects in danger

Insects! Millions and millions and MILLIONS of insects.

How many insects do you think there are in the world? One million? Two million? Are you surprised to hear there are over 30 MILLION insects in the world? That’s a lot of insects!

But the number of insects is falling fast. In fact, scientists think that 33% of insects are endangered – and perhaps as many as 25% could very soon become extinct! The most important reason for this? Well, humans are not taking care of the habitats where insects live. In fact, scientists say we are making their habitats smaller and less welcoming.

Humans do things every day which are terrible for insects. Cutting down trees and forests, causing pollution and climate change, and using pesticides on our crops and plants – all of these things are bad for insects and their homes.

So, why should we care about insects? You might ask this question when they are buzzing around your picnic in the summer! Insects are actually essential to our life on Earth. They have many important jobs to do: pollinating plants, helping to recycle dead plant and animal materials, and even providing food for animals, birds, and humans.

There are many reasons to care about insects, and many ways to help them. All insects really need is a safe place to live and lots of food. More plants means happier insects! Think about planting an insect-friendly garden at home, at school, or even work with your neighbours to make a community garden. Now that’s a bee-autiful idea!
