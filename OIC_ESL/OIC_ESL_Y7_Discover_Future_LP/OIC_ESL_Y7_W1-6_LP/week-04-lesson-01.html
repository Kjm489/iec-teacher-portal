Week 4, Lesson 1: How does the past shape the present?
Learning outcomes: 7.1d, 7.1e

Context
This is the first lesson in the unit ‘Learning from the past’.
Students will develop their listening skills by predicting content, then listening for main points and specific information in a video. They will practise the language of cause and effect to explore the impact of historical events on the world.
This lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, although the timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
