YEAR 7 Learning from the past
Week 5, Lesson 3: How can we find out more about the past?
Learning outcomes: 7.1d, 7.1e, 7.2e

Context
This is the eighth lesson in the unit ‘Learning from the past’.
Students will develop their listening and speaking skills by listening to a conversation for gist and specific information. They will continue to build on their research skills by creating appropriate questions and analysing the reliability of sources.
This lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, although the timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
