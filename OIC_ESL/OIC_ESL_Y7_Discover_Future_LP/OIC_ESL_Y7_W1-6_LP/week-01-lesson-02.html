6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min Support: Use a visual, short word bank and a claim–evidence sentence frame. Core: Give a complete explanation with one relevant detail. Challenge: Compare two examples of identity characteristics, evaluating the strength of the evidence. Check: Ask learners to underline the evidence in their response.

Oxford differentiation: Rather than whole-class discussions, divide the class into smaller groups of three or four students. Put less able or less confident students together with one more able student to act as a facilitator to encourage participation.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min Review: Invite two different responses and compare their supporting evidence. Record: I = independent; P = prompted; N = not yet.
