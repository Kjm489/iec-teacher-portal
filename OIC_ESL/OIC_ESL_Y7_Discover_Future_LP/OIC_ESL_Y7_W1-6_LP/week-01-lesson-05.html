Previous lesson directions (reference)

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min Partner task: In pairs, compare two source examples related to Who am I and explain a difference using evidence. Roles: A describes; B asks a follow-up; then swap. ICQ: Will both partners speak and switch roles? CCQ: What evidence shows a relevant example and supporting evidence?

Oxford main activity — supported collaboration: Provide a model about yourself. Read it aloud and then give students copies. This will give them an insight into your life and model the layout.

Collaborative scaffold (IEC): Discuss one response with a partner, explain your reasoning, and take turns.

YEAR 7 Identity

Learning outcomes: 7.4a, 7.4d, 7.4g

Context
This is the fifth lesson in the unit ‘Identity’.
Students will develop their writing skills by writing a profile of their ‘secret life’, then editing and revising their work. They will learn to cultivate empathy by sharing experiences and listening to their classmates’ secret lives.
This lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, although the timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
