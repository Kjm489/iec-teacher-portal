6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min Support: Use a visual, short word bank and a claim–evidence sentence frame. Core: Give a complete explanation with one relevant detail. Challenge: Compare two examples of communication, evaluating the strength of the evidence. Check: Ask learners to underline the evidence in their response.

Oxford differentiation: Less able students might prefer to write an informal email, whereas more able students can be challenged to use more sophisticated language in a formal email.

Oxford differentiation: Put less able students with more able students in pairwork.

Oxford additional task: Students can complete exs.1–4 in ODF SB 2 pp.34–35. Ask them to add more ideas to the columns on the board (e.g. incomplete sentences, emojis, text speak – LOL, imperatives).
