Introductory activity

Write the following headlines on the board: ‘A Dramatic Rescue from Icy River’ and ‘Cable Car Drama’.

In pairs, students create as many questions as possible about the headlines, using ‘Who’, ‘What’, ‘When’, ‘Where, ‘Why’, and ‘How’ (e.g. Who was in the cable car? Who was rescued from the icy river?).

Ask them to consider which types of photo they would use for the headlines.

As a class, share students’ questions and photo ideas.

Main activity

Provide a mindful moment for students to pause and take a few deep breaths before you start the next stage of the lesson. Today’s lesson involves a lot of instructions and activities, so a short pause will help to develop students’ attention and executive function.

Explain to students that they are going to find out more about the news stories through teamwork and a graphic organizer.

Divide the class into two groups: A and B. Give Group A the article about the cable car (ODF SB 2 p.44) and Group B the article about the rescue (ODF WB p.40). Ask them to read through their article and talk about how it is organized.

Invite students to share their ideas with the class:

Introduction: a short summary that explains who, what, where, when, why, how

Body: adds further detail to the question words and includes some quotations from people involved

Conclusion: explains the outcome, perhaps including a quotation from someone to sum up the ending

Give students a graphic organizer (a handout or copied from the board) so they can deconstruct their article.

The graphic organizer should consist of three rows: Introduction – with the question words listed; Body – with the same question words listed and quotations; Conclusion – summary of event and quotation.

In pairs (from the same group), students read the article again and complete the graphic organizer.

Put students into new pairs, one each from Groups A and B. Tell students to ask their questions from the introductory activity about the article that their partner has read. Students work together to understand each other’s articles.

As a class, discuss the role of the photos in both news articles and highlight the punctuation used in the quotations.

Additional task

Answer the questions in ODF SB 2 p.44. ex.2 and ODF WB 2 ex.3.

Learning review

Students have used headlines to make predictions about news articles and created questions they would like to know the answer to. They have deconstructed a news article using a graphic organizer, then identified the answers to their questions.

Differentiation

Put less able students together to create questions and deconstruct an article. This allows you to give them more support in a small group.
