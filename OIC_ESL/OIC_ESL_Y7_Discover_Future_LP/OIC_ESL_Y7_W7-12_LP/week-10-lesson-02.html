Texting can lead to saying things you regret.

It’s difficult to ignore texts.

Arguments can be resolved quickly by text.

Invite groups to share their ideas with the class. Establish that texting is a way of communicating with others, but there are both pros and cons to sending text messages.

Main activity

Give each group of three a handout with a list of statements about texting. Ask students to organize them into pros and cons. Ideas may include:

1. Texting is fun – you can use emojis, GIFS, and stickers to make messages stand out.

2. Messages are read and replied to immediately.

3. They are quick and concise.

4. They are not suitable for long-form communication.

5. Misunderstandings can happen easily.

6. It is a great way to stay in touch and connected.

7. It doesn’t allow you to show emotion.

8. Nothing is private.

9. You don’t need to add a formal greeting.

10. Once you send something, it is permanent, and others can do what they want with the information.

Encourage students to talk about each statement in turn, referring to their personal experiences to illustrate their points. See if they can add any more pros and cons.

Invite groups to share their ideas with the whole class and discuss.
