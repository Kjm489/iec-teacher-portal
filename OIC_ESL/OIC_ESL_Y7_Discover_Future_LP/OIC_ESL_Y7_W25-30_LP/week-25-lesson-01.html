Introductory activity

Display pictures of examples of different global issues which will be of interest and relevance to students. They should include the issues listed in ODF SB 2 p.81 ex.2, but may also include other issues relevant to your regional context.

Set a time limit. In pairs, tell students to write words they associate with each one on sticky notes, and to stick these on the pictures.

Allocate one student to each picture. In turn, invite them to read out their words. Discuss any of particular interest and teach the new vocabulary.

Main activity

Turn to ODF SB 2 p.81 ex.2. Check students understand the hypothetical situation before they discuss their ideas in pairs. Elicit some estimates in a whole-class discussion.

Play the Factflix video to check students’ answers. Allow them to discuss the content in pairs, then watch again. Elicit which pair estimated closest to the numbers given.

Ask: Why does Zach say at the end: ‘… that’s good for all of us living in our global village …’? Do we really live in a ‘global village’?

Turn to the key phrases in ODF SB 2 p.81 ex.5. Play the video again, asking students to identify each phrase as they hear it in the recording. Establish any ways in which the phrases used by Sofia and Zach are different to the ones in the book, and teach the grammar of each phrase.

Ask: How do Sofia and Zach feel about the topics they’re talking about? How do you know? Play the video again, pausing regularly so students can discuss in pairs:

any words or phrases Sofia and Zach use to express their feelings,

any ways Sofia and Zach use their voices to express their feelings (intonation).

Elicit ideas in a whole-class discussion, then practise the intonation patterns of the key phrases and any other phrases students identified.

In small groups, students discuss five or six topics of local, national, and international relevance. Alternatively, give students the situations in ODF SB 2 p.81 ex.5 to discuss. Groups decide which two problems they have most responsibility to try to change.

Additional tasks

Complete ex.1 in ODF SB 2 p.126.

Ask pairs of students to write a sentence using one of the phrases from ODF SB 2 p.81 ex.5. They practise saying only its intonation pattern (without the words). Each pair ‘says’ their intonation pattern for the rest of the class to try to guess the sentence.

Learning review

Students have discussed different global problems, then listened for specific details. They have identified how feelings are expressed in speech and practised this in group conversations.

Differentiation

To support less able students, give them the transcript to work with when identifying language from the video.

As an extension task for more able students, encourage them to identify any other useful expressions or phrases used in the video, and to use them in their discussions.
