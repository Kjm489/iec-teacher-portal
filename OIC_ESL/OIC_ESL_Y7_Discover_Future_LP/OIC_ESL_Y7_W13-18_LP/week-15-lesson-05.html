Context
This is the fifth lesson in the unit ‘Travel’.
In this lesson, students will develop their reading and writing skills by researching refugees and producing a fact file about their experience. They will learn how role models have a positive impact, and will explore the values of freedom, justice, and community.
This lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, although the timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
