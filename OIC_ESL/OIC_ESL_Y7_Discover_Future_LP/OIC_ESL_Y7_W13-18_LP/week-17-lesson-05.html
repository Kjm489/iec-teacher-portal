3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min WE DO — shared example: Choose a second example from the textbook about education and learning. Elicit one answer and its evidence; build a response together on the board. Teacher prompts: What does the source say or show? Which detail supports the answer? How can we improve the sentence? 4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min Partner task: In pairs, compare two source examples related to education and learning and explain a difference using evidence. Roles: A describes; B asks a follow-up; then swap. ICQ: Will both partners speak and switch roles? CCQ: What evidence shows evidence for a learning claim?

Oxford main activity — supported collaboration: In small groups, students take turns presenting their speech to their peers. If they feel able, see if they can look up from their notes from time to time and speak naturally, using gestures and filler words to support what they’re saying.

Collaborative scaffold (IEC): Discuss one response with a partner, explain your reasoning, and take turns.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min Support: Use a visual, short word bank and a claim–evidence sentence frame. Core: Give a complete explanation with one relevant detail. Challenge: Compare two examples of education and learning, evaluating the strength of the evidence. Check: Ask learners to underline the evidence in their response.

Oxford differentiation: Allow less confident students to present their speech in pairs, rather than in small groups. Alternatively, they can practise their delivery first in pairs before moving on to the small-group presentation.

Oxford differentiation: As an extension task for more able students, ask them to compile a revision list of useful topic vocabulary.

This is the 15th lesson in the unit ‘Travel’.
