PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

PERIOD 2 · 45 MINUTES — YOU DO, Differentiation, Review

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min YOU DO — independent task: Individually create a written, spoken or diagram response explaining evidence for a learning claim in the context of education and learning. Success check: Include an accurate example and one supporting detail. ICQ: Do you complete your answer independently before comparing? CCQ: Does a learning strategy explain how you will study, or only where you live?

Oxford main activity — independent application: Students discuss their answers in pairs, then share ideas with the whole class.

Oxford main activity — independent application: Encourage students to think about a new skill that they would be interested in learning, but not to tell their classmates what it is. It could be a skill that will help them in their chosen future career, or a skill learned for pleasure. Tell them to note down how they might be able to teach themselves this skill, drawing upon their learning from the listening activity (e.g. through online tutorials, through lots of practice, by studying a course outside of school, or by asking questions and sharing knowledge between friends).

Oxford main activity — independent application: Refer students to the key phrases used for talking about learning in ODF SB 2 p.65 ex.6.

Oxford main activity — independent application: In small groups, students take turns drawing a visual representation of their chosen skill for their classmates to guess. When a group member has guessed correctly, invite the student to explain why they would like to learn this skill and how they could achieve it.

Independent check (IEC): Complete an individual response demonstrating the specified Oxford outcome.

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Previous lesson directions (reference)

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min Support: Use a visual, short word bank and a claim–evidence sentence frame. Core: Give a complete explanation with one relevant detail. Challenge: Compare two examples of education and learning, evaluating the strength of the evidence. Check: Ask learners to underline the evidence in their response.

Oxford differentiation: For more support with the listening activity, create a matching activity. Write the names of the autodidacts on the board in one column, the skill they learned in a second column, and their learning strategy in a third column for students to match together.
