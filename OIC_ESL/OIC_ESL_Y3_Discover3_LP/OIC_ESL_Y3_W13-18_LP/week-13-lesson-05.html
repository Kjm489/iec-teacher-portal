LESSON INFORMATION & OBJECTIVES

Measurable Learning Objectives

Topic: Travelling back in time. OIC outcomes: 3.3g, 3.3i. Unit: Exploring the past.

Success criteria: Students use lesson vocabulary and skills to complete a meaningful supported task and produce independent evidence of learning.

PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford opening: Ask children: How different do you think you were two or three years ago? Can you think of any changes? What can you do now that you couldn’t do when you were small?

Oxford opening: Tell children to close their eyes and reflect on these discussion points quietly for a few minutes.

Oxford opening: Bring the class back together and encourage children to share their thoughts, if they are comfortable. Highlight that they are all growing into kind, caring, and smart individuals.

ICQ: Are we working independently or with our partner/group?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford task — teacher demonstration: Revise or teach the past simple. Explain that we use this tense to describe or talk about a completed action in the past. See Oxford Discover SB 2 p.53 and p.73 for reference. Write some common verbs (with regular and irregular past simple endings) in the present tense on the board. In turn, invite children to come to the front of the class and write the verb in the past simple underneath.

Oxford task — teacher demonstration: Hand out the story provided.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task aloud and visibly before inviting learner responses.

CCQ: What does the model communicate? Which words or clues help you understand it?

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford task — guided: Tell children that they are going to read about someone who travelled to the past in a time machine.

Oxford task — guided: Read the text as a whole class. Write any unknown words on the board and ensure children’s understanding.

ICQ: Will we complete the first example together before working alone?

CCQ: Which detail in the source activity supports your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford pair/group task: Ask a confident child to summarize the story in their own words for the benefit of their classmates. Write main points on the board.

Oxford pair/group task: Elicit the format of a comic strip. (It has a series of small frames with a different scene from a story illustrated in each frame. You read the dialogue in speech bubbles. Frames are numbered so you know in which order to read the story.) Encourage children to draw a three-frame comic strip to illustrate what happens in ‘My journey to the past’.

Scaffold: Use vocabulary from the Oxford task and provide a spoken sentence frame before removing support.

ICQ: Does each learner take a turn and listen to their partner?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford source task: Ask a confident child to summarize the story in their own words for the benefit of their classmates. Write main points on the board.

Oxford source task: Elicit the format of a comic strip. (It has a series of small frames with a different scene from a story illustrated in each frame. You read the dialogue in speech bubbles. Frames are numbered so you know in which order to read the story.) Encourage children to draw a three-frame comic strip to illustrate what happens in ‘My journey to the past’.

Independent evidence: Each learner completes or reports an individual response based on the Oxford activity, without copying the teacher model.

ICQ: Should you attempt your own response before asking for help?

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: Guide less confident children through the text in a small group. Ask them to identify examples of the past simple tense to check that they have grasped the new grammar. Give them a handout of common past simple verbs.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able children to continue the story, using a range of past simple verbs. Ask: What will happen next?

Oxford differentiation: Additional information: Week 13, Lesson 5

Oxford differentiation: My journey to the past

Oxford additional tasks: Encourage children to write their own time travel story, using the language they have learned this week.

IEC extension: Use the original Oxford task at a greater level of independence, or add a reason/example when learners are ready.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have revised or learned the past simple. They have read a story about travelling to the past. They have looked at the layout of a comic strip and identified the main steps involved in making one.

Formative assessment: Check the original OIC outcome through one independent spoken, written, or practical demonstration. Record independent / prompted / not yet.

CCQ: What evidence shows the learner understood the task?

SIX ACTIVITIES & GAMES — WEEK 13, LESSON 5

IEC supplemental game — Time travel passport

Purpose: Practise the Year 3 lesson focus: Travelling back in time.

Materials: Lesson textbook visuals, vocabulary cards, board and student notebooks.

How to play: Show a concrete example from the lesson. Learners observe, describe it using the target words, compare answers with a partner and justify one detail to the class.

ICQ: Do you describe the example before comparing answers?

CCQ: What in this example supports your description?

Assessment: Correct description and supporting detail

Support: Pre-teach 3–4 key words; provide a model, picture cues and a sentence starter.

Challenge: Ask learners to explain a second example or justify a different answer.

IEC supplemental game — Historical suitcase sort

Purpose: Practise the Year 3 lesson focus: Travelling back in time.

Materials: Lesson textbook visuals, vocabulary cards, board and student notebooks.

How to play: Give pairs 6–8 labelled picture/word cards drawn from the lesson. Learners sort or match them, explain one choice, then exchange sets to check.

ICQ: Are you sorting or writing a story? Do you explain one choice?

Assessment: Correct grouping plus a spoken reason

YEAR 3 Exploring the past
Week 13, Lesson 5: Travelling back in time
Learning outcomes: 3.3g, 3.3i

Context
This is the fifth lesson of the unit ‘Exploring the past’.
In this lesson, children will develop their reading skills by reading and understanding a story. They will look at how a comic strip is organized.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
