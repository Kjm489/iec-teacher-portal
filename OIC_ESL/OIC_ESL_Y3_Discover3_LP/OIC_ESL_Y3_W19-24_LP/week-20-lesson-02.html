PERIOD 1 — 45 MINUTES

1. Warm-up & Activate Prior Knowledge · 8 min

Oxford opening: Count around the room from 1 to 50 (or to the highest number the class knows in English). Challenge the class by counting backwards, in twos, and in threes.

ICQ: Are we working independently or with our partner/group?

2. Teacher Modeling — I DO · 12 min

Oxford task — teacher demonstration: Show the class a thermometer and elicit what it is and what it does. Encourage children to guess what the temperature is at the moment. Then invite a confident child to read the thermometer and tell you the temperature (in degrees).

Oxford task — teacher demonstration: Draw a thermometer on the board with a different temperature. Elicit the temperature. Ask: If it’s 30 degrees, is it summer or winter? Is it sunny or snowy? Spend time encouraging children to connect different temperatures to different weathers. Show the weather flashcards and elicit the weather words.

Oxford task — teacher demonstration: Now ask children what time the sun ‘rises’ and ‘sets’ in your location, introducing the new vocabulary. Take their predictions, then give them the correct answers.

Teacher modeling: Demonstrate the first source task aloud and visibly before inviting learner responses.

CCQ: What does the model communicate? Which words or clues help you understand it?

3. Guided Practice — WE DO · 15 min

Oxford task — guided: Turn to exs.A–B in Oxford Discover SB 3 p.74. Play the audio two or three times. Check answers in pairs first, then review as a class. For each location, ask children to listen for the following:

Oxford task — guided: Sunrise/sunset times

Oxford task — guided: What the weather will be like tomorrow

Oxford task — guided: High/low temperatures

ICQ: Will we complete the first example together before working alone?

CCQ: Which detail in the source activity supports your answer?

4. Supported Pair/Group Practice · 10 min

Oxford pair/group task: Turn to exs.A–B in Oxford Discover SB 3 p.74. Play the audio two or three times. Check answers in pairs first, then review as a class. For each location, ask children to listen for the following:

Oxford pair/group task: Form small groups. Allocate a different city to each group. Ask them to guess what they think the weather and the temperature (high and low) will be like there tomorrow and in three months’ time. To extend, they can also say what time the sun will rise/set.

Oxford pair/group task: Invite groups to write one of their sentences on the board. Ask the class if they can see any mistakes and peer-correct. Reinforce that making mistakes helps us to learn.

Scaffold: Use vocabulary from the Oxford task and provide a spoken sentence frame before removing support.

ICQ: Does each learner take a turn and listen to their partner?

PERIOD 2 — 45 MINUTES

5. Independent Practice — YOU DO · 25 min

Oxford source task: Additional information (e.g. if it’s the longest/shortest day)

Oxford source task: Form small groups. Allocate a different city to each group. Ask them to guess what they think the weather and the temperature (high and low) will be like there tomorrow and in three months’ time. To extend, they can also say what time the sun will rise/set.

Oxford source task: Tell them to work together to share their predictions in full sentences before checking them online. They can then alter their sentences to match the real forecast.

Oxford source task: Invite groups to write one of their sentences on the board. Ask the class if they can see any mistakes and peer-correct. Reinforce that making mistakes helps us to learn.

Independent evidence: Each learner completes or reports an individual response based on the Oxford activity, without copying the teacher model.

ICQ: Should you attempt your own response before asking for help?

6. Extension & Differentiation · 10 min

Oxford differentiation: To support less able children in the listening activity, provide a gapped text for them to complete or ask them to listen for the weather and temperature only.

Oxford differentiation: Challenge more able children to find a city that is/will be below freezing/over 40 degrees.

Oxford additional tasks: Complete exs.A–E in Oxford Discover WB 3 pp.63–64.

Oxford additional tasks: Write sentences about a different city.

IEC extension: Use the original Oxford task at a greater level of independence, or add a reason/example when learners are ready.

7. Review, Formative Assessment & Exit Ticket · 10 min

Oxford learning review: Children have made predictions about weather and temperature. They have recognized new topic vocabulary and identified main information in a weather forecast.

Formative assessment: Check the original OIC outcome through one independent spoken, written, or practical demonstration. Record independent / prompted / not yet.

CCQ: What evidence shows the learner understood the task?

SIX ACTIVITIES & GAMES — WEEK 20, LESSON 2

IEC supplemental game — Weather bulletin listening

Purpose: Practise the Year 3 lesson focus: Weather announcements.

Materials: Lesson textbook visuals, vocabulary cards, board and student notebooks.

How to play: Play or read a short lesson-related sound or utterance twice. Learners identify a key detail, compare answers in pairs, then explain which sound or word they heard.

ICQ: Do you listen twice? Do you compare with a partner before answering?

CCQ: Which word or sound helped you decide?

Assessment: Accurate listening response and evidence

Support: Pre-teach 3–4 key words; provide a model, picture cues and a sentence starter.

Challenge: Ask learners to explain a second example or justify a different answer.

IEC supplemental game — Weather symbol match

Purpose: Practise the Year 3 lesson focus: Weather announcements.

Materials: Lesson textbook visuals, vocabulary cards, board and student notebooks.

How to play: Give pairs 6–8 labelled picture/word cards drawn from the lesson. Learners sort or match them, explain one choice, then exchange sets to check.

ICQ: Are you sorting or writing a story? Do you explain one choice?

CCQ: Which feature tells you this card belongs in that group?

Assessment: Correct grouping plus a spoken reason

Support: Pre-teach 3–4 key words; provide a model, picture cues and a sentence starter.

Challenge: Ask learners to explain a second example or justify a different answer.

Context
This is the seventh lesson of the unit ‘Measuring time’.
In this lesson, children will develop their listening skills by listening to a weather forecast for main information.
The core lesson should take around 45 minutes to an hour, with extension activities provided for extending to 90 minutes over two lessons. The timing is flexible to suit different timetables.
