IEC TEACHER DEVELOPMENT ACADEMY

Professional Development Courses

Follow the four-level IEC professional development pathway: build foundational knowledge, apply expert classroom judgment, design an IEC TESOL lesson plan, and demonstrate competency through the final certification assessment.

LEVEL 1 · FOUNDATION

Effective Scaffolding

Learn It. Try It. Teach It.

5 interactive lessons · Classroom scenarios · ICQs & CCQs · Final knowledge assessment

Develop modeling, guided practice, differentiation, formative assessment, and student independence through practical interactive activities.

Start Course →
LEVEL 2 · ADVANCED APPLICATION

Advanced Teaching Mastery

Analyze It. Decide It. Defend It.

8 modules · 40 challenging classroom scenarios · Randomized responses · 80% passing score · Printable completion report

Apply TESOL/OIC principles to complex classroom evidence. Evaluate competing instructional decisions involving scaffolding, differentiation, ICQs and CCQs, student engagement, feedback, assessment, and lesson design. Choose the most defensible response when several strategies appear appropriate.

Start Advanced Mastery →
LEVEL 3 · PRACTICAL APPLICATION

IEC TESOL Lesson Planning Mastery

Learn It. Plan It. Submit It.

12 guided planning sections · 5 classroom scenarios · Fillable lesson plan · Printable academic review submission

Build a complete TESOL-oriented lesson plan with measurable learning objectives, warm-up, teacher modeling (I DO), guided practice (WE DO), independent application (YOU DO), ICQs, CCQs, differentiation, and formative assessment. Download or print the completed plan for academic review.

Start Lesson Planning Course →
LEVEL 4 · FINAL CERTIFICATION

International Teaching Excellence Certification

Prove It. Apply It. Demonstrate It.

75 advanced questions · 150 minutes · 80% passing score · Randomized questions · Printable completion report

Test your TESOL, OIC curriculum, scaffolding, differentiation, ICQ/CCQ, assessment, and classroom decision-making expertise. A separate TESOL-oriented lesson plan (minimum 80/100) must be approved for internal IEC certification.
