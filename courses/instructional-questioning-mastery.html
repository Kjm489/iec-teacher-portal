Instructional Questioning Mastery

Advanced ICQs, CCQs, diagnostic questioning, and evidence-based instructional decisions. Analyze realistic TESOL/OIC classroom situations where several responses appear reasonable.

8 modules · 40 scenarios · 80% pass (32/40)

Specialist Assessment

This is a focused professional development assessment, separate from the IEC four-level certification pathway. Every question presents four credible instructional decisions. Select the single most defensible response based on the precise evidence and constraints.

Rules: Answer all 40 questions. Questions and answer choices are randomized on each attempt. No answers are revealed until submission. The final report shows your overall score, eight module scores, your responses, the best answers, and rationales. Passing the assessment alone does not certify observed classroom practice.

PDF report: After submitting, select Download / Save Report as PDF and choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog. You can also print a paper copy.
