Course instructions

Complete the teacher information, work through all twelve guided lesson-planning sections, then complete the scenario checks. Review your work and Print / Save as PDF or Download completed HTML for academic review.

This is an internal IEC professional-development activity, not an external TESOL qualification. The scenario check is formative; academic approval is granted only after a reviewer evaluates the submitted plan.
