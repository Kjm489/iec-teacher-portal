Applied TESOL / OIC Classroom Decision-Making
8 modules · 40 advanced decision cases · 80% passing

Think like an expert teacher.

Analyze classroom evidence, evaluate realistic alternatives, and select the most defensible instructional response. Every option is intentionally plausible. Weigh the evidence, competing explanations, learner needs, and time constraints before choosing the single most defensible action.

Assessment rules: 40 challenging cases, one best answer each. There may be more than one reasonable action, but only one best-supported decision in context. Answer every question. Feedback and rationales appear after submitting the entire assessment. Passing requires at least 32/40. A separate applied TESOL lesson plan is required for academic review; passing the quiz alone does not constitute final certification.
