Certification requirements

Part A — Challenge Examination: At least 80% (60 of 75 points).

Part B — Original TESOL-oriented lesson plan: Separate written submission to the IEC Director of International Programs or authorized assessor. Minimum passing grade: 80/100.

Lesson plan must demonstrate OIC/content alignment, measurable language objectives, teacher modeling, scaffolding, differentiation, ICQs, CCQs, guided and independent practice, and formative assessment.

Certification status: PENDING ACADEMIC REVIEW. This report is not a certificate, teaching license, or externally accredited TESOL qualification. Results are generated locally in the teacher's browser and are not independently authenticated.
