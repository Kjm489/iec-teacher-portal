Welcome to Level 2

This foundation-to-application course prepares teachers to check task instructions (ICQs) and conceptual understanding (CCQs). Complete the instructional modules, practice with feedback, then take the final assessment.

ICQ

Instruction Checking Question: checks what learners do, with whom, how many, and when.

“Do we circle or write?”
CCQ

Concept Checking Question: checks meaning, distinctions, and reasoning.

“Can two different fractions represent the same amount?”
Do not rely on

“Do you understand?” or “Is that clear?” These rarely provide reliable evidence of understanding.

Certification note: This is IEC internal professional development, not an externally accredited qualification. Completion does not replace classroom observation.
